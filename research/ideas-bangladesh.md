# একটুখানি: tool ideas from what Bangladeshis need, search for and share

Prepared 10 October 2026. Builds on `research/trends-2026-10.md` (calendar, nostalgia, share drivers, SEO); does not repeat it. Excludes the 12 live tools and district-visited maps.

## How to read this

- **Evidence.** Every claim links a source. `ac "…"` links are live Google autocomplete for Bangladesh (`hl=bn&gl=bd`), captured 10 Oct 2026; open one to see the current list. The full capture (363 stems) is in the appendix. News links are to the article I read or the search result I could open.
- **Simple (1–5).** 5 = one obvious action (one tap or one field), result on the first screen. 4 = two or three inputs. 3 = several picks (for example 11 players). 2 = needs reading or a lot of typing. I left out ideas that would score 1.
- **Owner guidance applied.** Faith ideas count and display; they never rule (no "জায়েজ/হারাম" answers) and anything needing authoritative data is flagged. Hindu, Buddhist and Christian occasions sit beside Muslim ones. Cricket and Argentina–Brazil football get their own section. Ideas favour one common thing made playful over feature-heavy or niche tools.
- **Labels.** [hypothesis] = my product bet without direct demand evidence.

## What people share on Facebook, in one paragraph

The image is the share, and it works best when it holds **a number** (age, score, days left, GPA, ৳), **a side** (Argentina or Brazil, ইলিশ or চিংড়ি) or **a question the comments can answer** (a riddle, "কাকে ট্যাগ করবে?"). Bangladeshis already make these by hand: bKash says about 10 lakh people sent Eid salami with a digital card at Eid-ul-Fitr 2025, 30% of them on চাঁদরাত ([Jagonews24](https://www.jagonews24.com/economy/news/1101918)); a Transfermarkt Facebook reaction contest ended with Bangladesh's ❤️ beating Argentina's 🧡 by 800,000+ to about 16,000 ([Observer](https://observerbd.com/news/519518)); "photocard" is a word people search ([ac "ফটোকার্ড"](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=bn&gl=bd&q=ফটোকার্ড): ফটোকার্ড ডিজাইন, জেনারেটর, তৈরি), although news-branded photocards are a known fake-news vehicle ([Samakal, Sep 2026](https://samakal.com/bangladesh/article/375026/), [Desh Rupantor](https://www.deshrupantor.com/631359)). Caption hunting is enormous ([ac "ক্যাপশন"](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=bn&gl=bd&q=ক্যাপশন), [ac "স্ট্যাটাস"](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=bn&gl=bd&q=স্ট্যাটাস)), so every card should come with a ready line to paste. Two cautions from the autocomplete itself: even everyday games trigger "is it permissible?" searches ([ac "লুডু"](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=bn&gl=bd&q=লুডু): "লুডু খেলা কি হারাম"; [ac "ক্যারাম"](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=bn&gl=bd&q=ক্যারাম): "ক্যারাম খেলা কি হারাম"), and district-identity queries turn toxic fast ([ac "কোন জেলার"](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=bn&gl=bd&q=কোন%20জেলার): "কোন জেলার মেয়েরা বেশি সুন্দর").

## Strongest 10 (editorial)

Ranked by demand × commonness × simplicity × shareability:

1. **A1 আর্জেন্টিনা না ব্রাজিল?** One tap, a side, a national tally; the rivalry is the country's biggest football ritual.
2. **E1 বয়স কত? + Bangla birthday.** "বয়স ক্যালকুলেটর" is the top completion for বয়স; every birthday is a share.
3. **B4 সালামির চেক.** Autocomplete already asks for a salami cheque and card; the bKash numbers show the habit exists.
4. **C1 টাকা কথায় লিখি.** Dozens of exact-amount queries; evergreen utility, and it powers B4.
5. **B2 কত দিন বাকি?** Covers every faith's calendar; real "koto din baki" queries for Eid, Ramadan and three pujas.
6. **D1 জিপিএ কত?** HSC 2026 results land around 16 Oct; strong "gpa calculator hsc/ssc" demand.
7. **E3/E4 প্রবাসীর ঘড়ি ও দেশে ফেরার কাউন্টডাউন.** Strong diaspora queries; the link lets family at home watch.
8. **A2 আমার জার্সি.** "জার্সি নাম/নাম্বার ডিজাইন" is a real query; the output doubles as a profile picture.
9. **F2 নামের ডিজাইন.** Name-style queries are everywhere; Bangla lettering is a natural fit for the brand.
10. **G1 জ্যামে জীবনের কত দিন?** A painful, funny number every Dhaka commuter will post.
