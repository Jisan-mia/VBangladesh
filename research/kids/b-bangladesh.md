# Kids' section: what Bangladeshi children learn, love, need and live with

Researcher angle: Bangladeshi early-childhood education and culture, ages about 3–12.
Date: 2026-10-15. Sources are numbered `[n]` and listed at the end.
Owner update applied: international content is welcome. Every idea in §6 is tagged **BD**, **world** or **both**.

---

## 0. The short version

- Bangladeshi parents spend their worry on four school things: **যুক্তবর্ণ and reading fluency, handwriting, নামতা, and English**. National tests support the worry. In NSA 2022 only about half of Class 3 and Class 5 children reached grade level in Bangla, and only 39% (Class 3) and 30% (Class 5) did in maths [9][10].
- Children's top preventable killers are **drowning** (the leading cause of death at ages 1–4, mostly in a pond within 20 m of home [5]), **road crashes** (about 1,000 students a year, most aged 5–12 and on foot near home or school [6]), **burns** (highest at ages 1–4, mostly in the kitchen and from kupi lamps [13]), and **lightning** (about 270–350 deaths a year, children among the most exposed [20]). Dengue comes every monsoon [7].
- The safety content we can teach through play is **what to do**, never what goes wrong. Give a child a calm rule and a choice to practise ("call a grown-up and hold out a long stick"). Never show a child in trouble.
- Our safest cultural raw material is **folk ছড়া** (no author) and work by **authors who died before 1966**: Tagore, Sukumar Ray, Upendrakishore, Jogindranath Sarkar, Madanmohan Tarkalankar. **Nazrul and Jasimuddin are still in copyright** in Bangladesh (both died in 1976).
- What makes a game feel Bangladeshi: eating rice by hand, two-shift schools, the tiffin box, the মুদি দোকান, rickshaw and CNG, the pond, load-shedding, নানাবাড়ি, ঈদের সালামি, টাকা coins, Bangla digits, পানি, আম্মু/আব্বু, খালা/ফুপু/চাচা/মামা, the six seasons, and children from every faith and from the hills.

## 1. The curriculum (NCTB)

### 1.1 Where the curriculum stands in late 2026

- The competency-based curriculum reached pre-primary and Classes 1, 6 and 7 in 2023, and Classes 2, 3, 8 and 9 in 2024. Classes 4 and 5 were due in 2025 [1][2][3].
- The interim government reverted **secondary** education to the 2012 curriculum. **Primary kept the revised curriculum**, and the 2026 primary books follow it [3][4]. A wholly new curriculum is planned for about 2027–28 [4].
- The new primary approach brought **no school exams before Class 3**, continuous assessment, and more activity and less memorising [2]. Parents report that it feels vague, and many lean harder on coaching to fill the gap [11].
- **Takeaway for us:** design to the *skills*, which change slowly, not to chapter numbers or textbook wording, which change with governments. Never copy NCTB text or pictures. They are government copyright, and the books get revised. Never claim "NCTB-approved".

### 1.2 What children learn, by stage

| Stage | Ages | Subjects (government and most private Bangla-medium schools) |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-primary | 5+ (many start at 4 in kindergartens) | One integrated play-based book (*আমার বই*): letters and sounds, counting to 10–20, shapes, colours, body, family, hygiene, rhymes, drawing |
| Class 1–2 | 6–8 | Bangla, English, Maths |
| Class 3–5 | 8–11 | Bangla, English, Maths, Science (প্রাথমিক বিজ্ঞান), **বাংলাদেশ ও বিশ্বপরিচয়**, Religion and moral education (Islam, Hindu, Buddhist or Christian, by family faith), plus arts and PE [12] |
| Ebtedayee madrasa | 6–11 | The same core plus Arabic, Qur'an, Aqaid-Fiqh. Madrasa students score lowest in the national tests [10] |

Note: English is compulsory from Class 1, but most children leave primary weak in writing and speaking it [11].

**Bangla (Class 1–3):** স্বরবর্ণ (11) and ব্যঞ্জনবর্ণ (39); কার-চিহ্ন (vowel signs) and ফলা (য-ফলা, র-ফলা, ব-ফলা and so on); **যুক্তবর্ণ** (conjuncts; the classic hurdle in Class 2–3); reading short passages aloud; rhymes and poems; writing with মাত্রা. In Class 3–5: reading comprehension, short compositions, letters and applications, idioms, synonyms and opposites.

**English:** alphabet in capital and small letters, phonics-ish sounds, simple words (the "A for Apple" tradition is universal), greetings, numbers, days and months, short sentences, and simple present tense by Class 3–5.

**Maths:** counting and Bangla digits ০–৯ → place value up to লাখ/কোটি (the Bangladeshi system, not millions) → the four operations → **নামতা** (tables to 10, often drilled to 20 at home) → fractions (ভগ্নাংশ), money (টাকা-পয়সা), time and clock, measurement (মিটার, কেজি, লিটার), simple geometry, and by Class 5 simple averages and percentages.

**Science (Class 3–5):** living and non-living things; plants and animals; food and nutrition (the balanced plate); the body and health; hygiene; water and air; matter (solid, liquid, gas); simple forces; weather and seasons; the solar system (Class 4–5, under "general science" [12]); the environment and pollution; natural disasters; electricity safety.

**বাংলাদেশ ও বিশ্বপরিচয় (Class 3–5):** family and neighbours; school and community; rules and good citizenship; **rights of the child**; the map of Bangladesh (divisions, districts, rivers); population and the many peoples of Bangladesh (Bangalee and small ethnic groups); national symbols; history (1952 Language Movement, 1971 Liberation War, independence days); our neighbours and the world; climate change and disasters.

**Religion and moral education:** stories of honesty, kindness, respect for parents and teachers, cleanliness, and helping others. It is taught separately by faith. **For us: the universal values are fair game, but any faith-specific teaching is not.**

### 1.3 What parents actually worry about (ranked by how often it comes up)

1. **Reading fluency and যুক্তবর্ণ.** The child "knows letters but can't read a word with ক্ষ/ঙ্গ/ন্ত". This is the single biggest Class 1–3 worry, and NSA data backs it [9][10].
2. **Handwriting.** Messy letters, missing মাত্রা, wrong stroke order, mirrored digits (৪/৮, ৭/৯). Many parents still judge schooling by the neatness of the খাতা.
3. **নামতা.** Rote recitation is the parental benchmark ("৭-এর ঘর পারে?"). The site already has *namta*.
4. **English.** Alphabet, small letters (b/d/p/q confusion), spelling, and "speaking". Many parents can't help with it themselves, which drives coaching [11].
5. **Maths word problems and money.** Class 3–5.
6. **Screen time itself.** Parents feel guilty about YouTube. A tool that is plainly educational, short and ad-free earns their trust, and the cheap phone is the family's shared phone.

## 2. Life-skills and values children already know (themes only)

**Meena (UNICEF, since 1992)** has covered: girls' right to school; boys and girls getting **equal food and equal play**; sharing household chores; against child marriage and dowry; **latrines, handwashing and clean water**; caring for younger siblings; vaccinations; the dangers of trafficking; bullying [15][16]. In Bangladesh, Meena stories went into the school curriculum [15]. Parents and teachers aged 20–45 grew up with it, so these values will *feel familiar*.

**Sisimpur (Sesame Workshop Bangladesh, since 2005)** covers literacy and numeracy; **handwashing, tooth brushing and safe water** (a WASH pilot was evaluated in Bangladesh and India [8]); emotions and self-care; **diversity, inclusion and disability** (recent seasons added an autistic character and sign language); the environment; empathy and cooperation; road safety; and festivals of every faith [8][14].

**Use:** equal treatment of girls and boys, sharing, kindness to animals, hygiene, road safety, inclusion of children with disabilities, respect for all work, caring for the environment, emotions.
**Do not use:** any character, name, look-alike design, signature phrase or song from either show. Write original characters with ordinary Bangladeshi names.

## 3. Child safety and health in Bangladesh

| Hazard | Evidence | What a child can actually do | Play-safe framing |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Drowning** | Leading cause of death at ages 1–4; about 80% in ponds, ditches or buckets within 20 m of home; 97% of child victims could not swim [5]. SwimSafe has taught 250,000+ children aged 4–16 [17] | Ages 3–5: little. This message is for **parents** (watch under-5s, fence the pond, use a crèche). Ages 6–12: never go to the pond alone; **call a grown-up; reach with a stick or gamchha, or throw a bottle; don't jump in** (SwimSafe's land-based rescue [17]); learn to swim with an adult | A ball rolls toward the পুকুরঘাট and the child picks the right choice. Never show anyone in the water struggling. Add a parent note with the facts |
| **Road** | About 1,000+ student deaths a year; most child victims aged 5–12 are **walking near home or school**, hit by auto-rickshaws, motorbikes or goods vehicles, often on rural roads [6] | Hold a grown-up's hand; **look right first** (Bangladesh drives on the left), then left, then right again; use the zebra crossing or foot-over-bridge; never run after a ball into the road; get off a rickshaw on the footpath side | A crossing game with rickshaw, CNG, bus and motorbike. The "wrong" move simply means "wait"; no crash animation |
| **Burns** | Highest at ages 1–4; mostly in the kitchen while food is cooked; kupi (kerosene lamp) is a major risk; homes without a separate kitchen face higher risk [13] | Stay away from the চুলা, hot pots, the kettle and the iron; don't play with matches or kupi; **থামো-শুয়ে পড়ো-গড়াও** if clothes catch fire; cool a burn under running water | "Hot or not?" sorting in a kitchen scene |
| **Kite string and wires** | Repeated cases of children electrocuted on roofs or balconies by power lines while flying kites, and by illegal live wires [18] | Fly kites only in open fields; never pull a kite caught on a wire; stay away from rooftop wires; don't use metallic or glass-coated string | A pre-flight check in the site's existing kite games (*ghuri*, *vo-katta*) |
| **Lightning** | About 270–350 deaths a year, mostly rural; children are among the most at risk; two 13-year-olds were killed while flying kites in 2025 [20] | Go indoors when thunder starts; don't stand under a lone tree or in an open field or water; crouch low if caught out | Part of a storm-ready game; calm, matter-of-fact |
| **Dengue** | Every monsoon: 82,600 cases and 326 deaths in 2025 by mid-November [7] | Find and tip out standing water (flower-pot trays, coconut shells, tyres, fridge trays, AC drip); sleep under a মশারি; tell a parent about fever | Hidden-object hunt: "find the mosquitoes' water". Tidying, not fear |
| **Handwashing / safe water** | Soap before food prep and after the toilet is tied to much less child diarrhoea [19]. People over-report soap use (14% said, under 1% did) [19] | Soap at the critical times: before eating (**we eat rice by hand**), after the toilet, after play; drink only safe water | A scrubbing rhythm game, 20 seconds |
| **Teeth** | Local studies find caries in about 40–65% of school children; many brush only once, before breakfast [21] | Brush morning and **before bed**, for 2 minutes, with fluoride paste; fewer sweets between meals | Brushing timer with germs leaving |
| **Getting lost / strangers / body safety** | Crowds at মেলা, ঈদ markets, launch ghats. Body-safety education is done by NGOs and is sensitive | Know a parent's phone number by heart; if lost, stand still and ask a mother with children or a shopkeeper; tell a trusted adult if anything feels bad; surprises are fine but secrets that make you feel bad are not | Phone-number memory game; "who are my trusted people" circle. Do **not** depict abuse; keep wording general and add a parent note |
| **Floods and cyclones** | Coastal and char children live with signal numbers and shelters | Know the family's emergency bag; go to the shelter early with family | Pack-the-bag sorting game |

**Rules for all safety content:**
1. Show the safe action, not the harm. No death, injury, drowning, fire or crash animations.
2. Treat a wrong answer as "let's try again" (*আরেকবার ভাবো*), never as blame.
3. Every safety game carries a short **parent note** with one fact and one action. For under-5s the real protection is adults.
4. Use no images of real victims and no news photos.

## 4. Culture

### 4.1 Rhymes (ছড়া): what is safe to use

Copyright in Bangladesh lasts the author's **life + 60 years** under the Copyright Act 2023 [22]. Folk rhymes with no known author are free to use. Tagore's essay *ছেলেভুলানো ছড়া* (1894) and Jogindranath Sarkar's *খুকুমণির ছড়া* (1899) are the classic records that show these rhymes were already old folk rhymes over a century ago [23][24].

**Safe to use verbatim (folk; no author):**
- আয় আয় চাঁদ মামা, টিপ দিয়ে যা
- ঘুম পাড়ানি মাসি পিসি, মোদের বাড়ি এসো
- খোকা ঘুমালো পাড়া জুড়ালো, বর্গী এল দেশে
- আগডুম বাগডুম ঘোড়াডুম সাজে
- নোটন নোটন পায়রাগুলি ঝোটন বেঁধেছে
- তাই তাই তাই, মামা বাড়ি যাই
- বৃষ্টি পড়ে টাপুর টুপুর, নদেয় এল বান
- ইকড়ি মিকড়ি চাম চিকড়ি (a counting-out rhyme)
- আতা গাছে তোতা পাখি, ডালিম গাছে মৌ
- চাঁদ উঠেছে ফুল ফুটেছে, কদম তলায় কে
- ওপেন্টি বায়োস্কোপ (a game chant)
- হাট্টিমা টিম টিম (folk, and also printed by Jogindranath, who died in 1937, so safe either way)

Caution: folk rhymes exist in many regional variants. Pick one common Bangladeshi version and stick with it. Some lines carry old social content (for example the বর্গী raid in খোকা ঘুমালো; some mention a bride's dowry). Use the gentle opening lines, or retell them.

**Safe: named authors who died before 1966.** Rabindranath Tagore (died 1941; *আমাদের ছোট নদী*, *সহজ পাঠ*), Sukumar Ray (1923; *আবোল তাবোল*), Upendrakishore Raychowdhury (1915; *টুনটুনির বই*), Jogindranath Sarkar (1937), Madanmohan Tarkalankar (1858; *পাখী সব করে রব*), Ishwar Chandra Vidyasagar (1891; *বর্ণপরিচয়*), Kamini Roy (1933), Satyendranath Dutta (1922), Dakshinaranjan Mitra Majumdar (1957; *ঠাকুরমার ঝুলি*), Kusumkumari Das (1948).

**Not safe (in copyright; quote a short line at most, or better, link to it):**
- **Kazi Nazrul Islam** (died 1976; free from 2037): *ভোর হলো দোর খোলো*, *লিচু চোর*, *খুকী ও কাঠবিড়ালি*
- **Jasimuddin** (died 1976): *মামার বাড়ি* (আয় ছেলেরা আয় মেয়েরা)
- **Bande Ali Mia** (died 1979): *আমাদের গ্রাম*
- Sukumar Barua, Rokanuzzaman Khan Dadabhai, Ahsan Habib, Shamsur Rahman, Humayun Ahmed, Muhammed Zafar Iqbal, and all living writers
- Modern children's songs and any TV jingles

This is my reading of the term, not legal advice. When in doubt, use folk material or write an original rhyme.

### 4.2 Riddles and word play
Folk ধাঁধা are everywhere (the site already has *dhadha*). Children also love tongue twisters (কাঁচা গাব পাকা গাব), *অন্ত্যমিল* games (rhyming words), and word chains (শব্দের শেষ অক্ষর দিয়ে নতুন শব্দ).

### 4.3 Children's games of Bangladesh
Outdoor: **কানামাছি, গোল্লাছুট, এক্কাদোক্কা, বউচি, দাড়িয়াবান্ধা, কুতকুত, লুকোচুরি, রুমাল চুরি, হাডুডু/কাবাডি (the national game), ইচিং বিচিং, ওপেন্টি বায়োস্কোপ, ফুল টোক্কা, মোরগ লড়াই**.
Indoor and board games: **ষোলোগুটি, বাঘবন্দি, লুডু, ক্যারম, কড়ি খেলা, পুতুল বিয়ে, রান্নাবাটি/চড়ুইভাতি**.
The site already has ডাংগুলি, চোর-পুলিশ, বাঘবন্দি, লাটিম, সাত চাড়া, রশি টানাটানি, মার্বেল, ক্যারম, সাপলুডু and ঘুড়ি. Still open: **এক্কাদোক্কা, ষোলোগুটি, কানামাছি, রান্নাবাটি, কড়ি খেলা, রুমাল চুরি**.

### 4.4 Festivals of every faith (include all, rule on none)
- **Islam:** ঈদুল ফিতর (new clothes, সালামি, সেমাই), ঈদুল আযহা (cattle hat, sharing meat with neighbours and the poor), রোজা and ইফতার, শবে বরাত (হালুয়া-রুটি in many homes)
- **Hindu:** দুর্গাপূজা (ঢাক, মণ্ডপ, মেলা), **সরস্বতী পূজা (students' festival, books and হাতেখড়ি)**, লক্ষ্মী পূজা, দীপাবলি/কালীপূজা (প্রদীপ), জন্মাষ্টমী, রথযাত্রা
- **Buddhist:** বুদ্ধ পূর্ণিমা, প্রবারণা পূর্ণিমা (**ফানুস**), কঠিন চীবর দান
- **Christian:** বড়দিন (তারা, কেক, গির্জা, কীর্তন), ইস্টার
- **Hill and indigenous peoples:** **বৈসাবি** (বৈসু-সাংগ্রাই-বিজু; ফুল ভাসানো, water play), ওয়ানগালা (Garo harvest)
- **Shared Bangali and national days:** পহেলা বৈশাখ (পান্তা-ইলিশ, মেলা, হালখাতা, মুখোশ), পহেলা ফাল্গুন/বসন্ত উৎসব, নবান্ন, পৌষ সংক্রান্তি/সাকরাইন (Old Dhaka kite festival), চৈত্র সংক্রান্তি, ২১ ফেব্রুয়ারি (শহীদ মিনার, flowers, barefoot), ২৬ মার্চ, ১৬ ডিসেম্বর

**Faith cautions:** show symbols and food, never deities or prophets as images. Don't rule on moon sighting or prayer times. Give every faith the same visual weight; a Hindu, Buddhist or Christian child should find their festival in any festival game. Avoid pork, alcohol and dog-heavy scenes. Keep clothing modest.

### 4.5 Seasons, nature and symbols
**ষড়ঋতু** (two Bangla months each): গ্রীষ্ম (বৈশাখ-জ্যৈষ্ঠ: আম-কাঁঠাল, কালবৈশাখী), বর্ষা (আষাঢ়-শ্রাবণ: কদম, নৌকা, কাদা), শরৎ (ভাদ্র-আশ্বিন: কাশফুল, সাদা মেঘ, শিউলি, পূজা), হেমন্ত (কার্তিক-অগ্রহায়ণ: নতুন ধান, নবান্ন), শীত (পৌষ-মাঘ: খেজুরের রস, ভাপা পিঠা, কুয়াশা), বসন্ত (ফাল্গুন-চৈত্র: কোকিল, শিমুল-পলাশ).

**National symbols** [25]: শাপলা (flower), কাঁঠাল (fruit), দোয়েল (bird), রয়েল বেঙ্গল টাইগার (animal), ইলিশ (fish), আমগাছ (tree), plus the flag, the emblem, the anthem (*আমার সোনার বাংলা*, by Tagore, so public domain; play it only with respect, never as a game sound), শহীদ মিনার and জাতীয় স্মৃতিসৌধ.

**Animals:** বাঘ, চিত্রা হরিণ, হাতি (hills), শিয়াল, বানর, বেজি, গুইসাপ, মেছোবাঘ, **শুশুক** (river dolphin), কুমির (Sundarbans), গরু-ছাগল-মহিষ, হাঁস-মুরগি, বিড়াল.
**Birds:** দোয়েল, কোকিল, ময়না, টিয়া, শালিক, চড়ুই, কাক, বক, মাছরাঙা, **বাবুই** (hanging nest), ঘুঘু, পেঁচা, কাঠঠোকরা, পানকৌড়ি.
**Fish:** ইলিশ, রুই, কাতলা, মৃগেল, পাবদা, কই, শিং, মাগুর, পুঁটি, টেংরা, বোয়াল, চিংড়ি.
**Fruits:** আম, কাঁঠাল, লিচু, জাম, কলা, পেঁপে, আনারস, পেয়ারা, তাল, নারকেল, বেল, কামরাঙা, আমড়া, বরই, জাম্বুরা, তেঁতুল, চালতা, ডালিম.
**Flowers:** শাপলা, শিউলি, কদম, কৃষ্ণচূড়া, বকুল, রজনীগন্ধা, গাঁদা, জবা, হাসনাহেনা, শিমুল, পলাশ, কাশফুল.
**Trees:** আম, বট, অশ্বত্থ, নারকেল, সুপারি, তাল, হিজল, শিমুল, সুন্দরী, গোলপাতা, কদম, বাঁশ.
**Rivers:** পদ্মা, মেঘনা, যমুনা, ব্রহ্মপুত্র, কর্ণফুলী, তিস্তা, সুরমা, বুড়িগঙ্গা, মধুমতী, শীতলক্ষ্যা. Also places children hear about: সুন্দরবন, কক্সবাজার (longest sea beach), সেন্ট মার্টিন, সাজেক, রাতারগুল, পাহাড়পুর, ষাটগম্বুজ মসজিদ.

## 5. A Bangladeshi child's day

**Urban (Dhaka, Chattogram, district towns):**
- Woken early; many Muslim children go to **মক্তব/আরবি পড়া** at dawn, or have a হুজুর come home
- Government primary runs in **morning and day shifts**; uniform, assembly with the anthem and the শপথ
- **Tiffin box**: রুটি-ভাজি, ডিম, নুডলস, পরোটা, বিস্কুট, or টাকা for the school-gate ঝালমুড়ি/চটপটি/আচার vendor
- Reaches school on foot, by rickshaw (often a monthly "school rickshaw"), van, or a parent's motorbike. Few school buses
- Afternoon: **coaching or a প্রাইভেট স্যার/ম্যাডাম**, homework with আম্মু, a nap
- Playtime is squeezed: the ছাদ, the গলি (cricket, badminton), a building's parking lot. Cartoons and YouTube on a parent's phone
- Evening: পড়তে বসা; load-shedding with a charger light; dinner together on the floor or at a table; grandparents' stories

**Rural and char:**
- Walk to school along আল and village roads; boat to school in some areas
- Helping with ছাগল-গরু, হাঁস-মুরগি, fetching water from the টিউবওয়েল; bathing in the pond (the drowning risk)
- Open fields for গোল্লাছুট, ঘুড়ি, ফুটবল; fruit trees to climb; the মেলা after harvest
- Weather defines life: flood season, কালবৈশাখী, শীতের সকাল with খেজুরের রস

**Family:** often joint or near-joint. দাদা-দাদি/নানা-নানি matter; চাচা, ফুপু, খালা, মামা are distinct people (the site already has *attiyo*). Many children have a **প্রবাসী** father or uncle in the Gulf, Malaysia, Italy or the UK, so time zones and video calls are part of life. Holidays mean **নানাবাড়ি/দাদাবাড়ি**.

**What makes content feel Bangladeshi, not translated:**
- **Words:** পানি, আম্মু/আব্বু (and মা/বাবা), খালা/ফুপু/চাচা/মামা, টিফিন, স্যার/ম্যাডাম, ছাদ, গলি, মুদি দোকান. Avoid Kolkata-isms (জলখাবার, ইস্কুল) and stiff সাধু forms. Note that Hindu families say জল. Don't police it, but default to Bangladeshi usage
- **Things:** টাকা coins and notes, rickshaw/CNG/launch/bus, the tube-well, পুকুরঘাট, the মশারি, ভাত-ডাল-মাছ eaten by hand, চানাচুর, পিঠা, the লুঙ্গি/শাড়ি/সালোয়ার-কামিজ, red-green flag colours
- **Numbers:** Bangla digits; লাখ/কোটি; ১, ২, ৫ টাকা coins; ১০, ২০, ৫০, ১০০ notes
- **Names:** mixed and inclusive. Rafi, Nusrat, Tanvir, Mim, Arpita, Joy, Priyanka, Mong, Chingla, Sanjida, Rony, Ritu
- **Western tells to avoid:** snow, Halloween, pumpkins, fireplaces, yellow school buses, cereal, sandwiches in lunchboxes, Christmas as the only festival, pet dogs as the default pet, "mom/dad" in a Bangla script, ice skating, apples as the only fruit

**Other sensitivities:**
- **Gender:** show girls and boys doing everything, including chores, sport and science.
- **Skin colour:** never tie beauty to ফর্সা skin.
- **Disability:** include a child in a wheelchair or with glasses as a normal part of scenes.
- **Indigenous peoples:** show hill and plains peoples respectfully, as neighbours, not as costumes.
- **Politics:** history content is contested between governments. Textbooks were rewritten in 2025 [3], and the পহেলা বৈশাখ procession was renamed in 2025. Stick to dates, places, symbols, the Language Movement and the Liberation War as shared events. **No portraits or ranking of political leaders**, and nothing on 2024 politics.
- **Music:** some conservative families avoid music. Every game must work fully on mute. Sound effects should be soft synthesized blips, with melody optional.

## 6. Game and tool ideas

Format: **name** · ages · tag · build effort (S = a day or two, M = about a week, L = more).
The build is a single-page browser game with synthesized sound only, no server, no accounts, no camera or microphone.
Bangla text-to-speech (`speechSynthesis`, bn-BD) exists on many Android phones but not reliably, so no idea depends on it.

### 6.1 Safety and health (BD)

1. **পুকুরপাড়ে সাবধান** · 6–10 · BD · M
   - **Builds:** drowning awareness and SwimSafe's land-based rescue (reach or throw, call for help).
   - **Loop:** short scenes at the পুকুরঘাট, canal or launch ghat. Pick what to do (call আম্মু, hold out a বাঁশ/গামছা, throw an empty bottle, never go alone), and the scene calmly resolves.
   - **Feels BD:** the pond is 20 m from the house.
   - **Also:** a parent card on watching under-5s and learning to swim with an adult. No struggling-in-water imagery.
2. **রাস্তা পার হই** · 5–10 · BD · M
   - **Builds:** road safety and impulse control.
   - **Loop:** guide a child across a road with rickshaws, CNGs, buses and motorbikes. **Look right first** (left-hand traffic), wait for the gap, use the zebra crossing or foot-over-bridge. Levels add a school gate, a ball rolling out, and getting down from a rickshaw.
   - **Feels BD:** the vehicle mix and the rickshaw get-down. A wrong move only means "wait".
3. **জমা পানি খুঁজি** · 5–12 · BD · M
   - **Builds:** dengue prevention and visual search.
   - **Loop:** a ছাদ/বারান্দা/উঠোন scene. Tap the hidden places where water collects (টবের প্লেট, ডাবের খোসা, টায়ার, AC drip, ফ্রিজের ট্রে) to tip them out, and hang the মশারি at the end.
   - **Feels BD:** monsoon rooftops. Different from the existing *moshar-raat* (swatting).
4. **সাবান-পানি** · 3–7 · BD · S
   - **Builds:** handwashing technique and the critical times.
   - **Loop:** a 20-second synthesized rhythm. Swipe palms, backs, between fingers, thumbs and nails while germs fade, then pick "when to wash" (before ভাত, after the toilet, after play).
   - **Feels BD:** eating rice by hand; the tube-well and the বদনা.
5. **দাঁত ঝকঝক** · 3–7 · BD · S
   - **Builds:** brushing routine (2 minutes, morning and bedtime).
   - **Loop:** brush each tooth zone on a cartoon mouth with a timer; the germs leave; earn a star for the bedtime brush.
   - **Feels BD:** the sweets temptations (চকলেট, আচার, মিষ্টি) appear as the "after this, brush" moment.
6. **গরম! ছুঁয়ো না** · 3–8 · BD · S
   - **Builds:** burn prevention.
   - **Loop:** in a kitchen scene, sort hot and safe things (মাটির চুলা, গ্যাসের চুলা, কুপি, ভাতের হাঁড়ি, কেটলি, ইস্ত্রি). A bonus round practises "থামো-শুয়ে পড়ো-গড়াও" as three taps in order.
   - **Feels BD:** the চুলা and the kupi.
7. **আম্মুর নম্বর** · 4–8 · BD · S
   - **Builds:** memorising a parent's mobile number (the lost-child lifeline) and working memory.
   - **Loop:** a parent types the number once (never saved or sent). The child rebuilds it in Bangla digits in growing chunks (০১৭.., then more) with a rhythm.
   - **Feels BD:** ঈদের বাজার and মেলা crowds; the 11-digit 01X numbers.
8. **আমার ভরসার মানুষ** · 5–10 · BD · M
   - **Builds:** naming trusted adults; secrets versus surprises; what to do if lost.
   - **Loop:** the child places people (আম্মু, নানু, শিক্ষক, খালা…) into a "ভরসার বৃত্ত". Then short scenarios: lost at the মেলা means stand still and ask a mother with children or a shopkeeper; a secret that makes you feel bad means tell someone in the circle.
   - **Also:** gentle wording and a parent note. No abuse depicted.
9. **ঝড়-বাদলের দিন** · 7–12 · BD · S
   - **Builds:** lightning and storm safety; disaster preparedness.
   - **Loop:** thunder starts in a field, so pick safe moves (go into a building, not under a lone tree, out of the pond). Then pack a বন্যা/ঘূর্ণিঝড় bag (পানি, শুকনো খাবার, টর্চ, ওষুধ, জরুরি কাগজ).
   - **Feels BD:** কালবৈশাখী and the coastal signal flags.
10. **ঘুড়ি ওড়ানোর আগে** · 6–12 · BD · S (an add-on to the existing *ghuri*/*vo-katta*)
    - **Builds:** kite-and-wire safety.
    - **Loop:** before a kite game starts, pick the safe place (open মাঠ, not a ছাদ next to wires). A kite stuck on a wire means let it go and tell a grown-up.

### 6.2 Reading and writing (BD)

11. **যুক্তবর্ণ ভাঙি-জুড়ি** · 6–10 · BD · M
    - **Builds:** conjunct letters, parents' number-one worry.
    - **Loop:** a picture-word appears (পদ্ম, বৃষ্টি, চন্দ্র, স্কুল, পাখি→পক্ষী); drag the two or three base letters to build the যুক্তবর্ণ, or split one into its parts. Levels go from easy (ক্ক, ম্ম) to hard (ক্ষ, ঞ্জ, হ্ম).
    - **Feels BD:** the words come from BD life.
12. **কার-চিহ্ন লাগাই** · 5–8 · BD · M
    - **Builds:** vowel signs and decoding.
    - **Loop:** a consonant plus a picture. Snap on the right কার (া ি ী ু ূ ৃ ে ৈ ো ৌ) to make the word (কলা, মাছ, ঘুড়ি, নৌকা). A বানান-ঠিক sound plays.
13. **হাতের লেখা** · 4–7 · BD · L
    - **Builds:** stroke order, letter shape, মাত্রা last; digit confusion (৪/৮, ৭/৯).
    - **Loop:** finger-trace a letter along guided strokes, scored on order and closeness; the মাত্রা is drawn last. English capitals and smalls (b/d/p/q) come later.
    - **Effort:** L because of the stroke data for 50 letters plus digits.
    - **Note:** this is not a replacement for pencil practice; say so to parents.
14. **ছোট্ট গল্প পড়ি** · 6–9 · BD · M (content-heavy)
    - **Builds:** reading fluency and comprehension.
    - **Loop:** levelled 5–8 sentence original stories about BD life (টিফিন হারানো, নানাবাড়ির বিড়াল). Tap a word to see it split into syllables. Two picture questions at the end. Levels add যুক্তবর্ণ gradually.
15. **ছড়ার তাল** · 3–7 · BD · S
    - **Builds:** phonological awareness, rhythm and rhyme.
    - **Loop:** a public-domain folk ছড়া scrolls line by line. Tap along to a soft synthesized ডুগডুগি beat, then pick the missing rhyming word (চাঁদ মামা… টিপ দিয়ে যা).
    - **Feels BD:** every BD child knows these rhymes. Works on mute.
16. **অক্ষরের রেলগাড়ি** · 4–6 · BD · S
    - **Builds:** letter order and recognition.
    - **Loop:** a train of স্বরবর্ণ or ব্যঞ্জনবর্ণ carriages has gaps; drag the missing letter in and the train toots (synthesized) and moves.
17. **ABC বাজার** · 4–7 · both · S/M
    - **Builds:** the English alphabet in capitals and smalls, plus initial sounds.
    - **Loop:** a market stall. Match "M m" to the mango, "B b" to the boat, "F f" to the fish; then pair capital with small letters.
    - **Feels BD:** English words for BD things (Boat, Mango, Rickshaw, Jackfruit).

### 6.3 Maths (BD)

18. **মুদি দোকানে কেনাকাটা** · 6–9 · BD · M
    - **Builds:** money, addition and subtraction.
    - **Loop:** buy two items from a list at the মুদি দোকান using ১, ২, ৫ টাকা coins and ১০, ২০, ৫০ notes, pay exactly, then count change.
    - **Feels BD:** real BD money and prices.
    - **Note:** the existing *bhangti-nai* is an adult-pace change game. This one is slower and has pictures.
19. **ঘড়ি দেখি** · 6–9 · BD · S/M
    - **Builds:** reading an analogue clock and time words.
    - **Loop:** a day's routine (স্কুল সকাল ৭টা, টিফিন, বিকেলে খেলা, পড়তে বসা, ঘুম). Set the clock hands to match, or read the time to pick the activity.
    - **Feels BD:** the two-shift school day.
20. **সমান ভাগ** · 6–10 · BD · M
    - **Builds:** fractions and sharing fairly (a value and a maths skill at once).
    - **Loop:** cut a পিঠা, রুটি or তরমুজ, or split a bowl of কাঁঠালের কোয়া, so siblings and cousins get equal shares; ½, ⅓, ¼ appear as you cut.
21. **এক্কাদোক্কা** · 4–8 · BD · M
    - **Builds:** counting, number order and timing.
    - **Loop:** throw the ঘুঁটি onto a numbered box (a timing tap), then hop the boxes in order while skipping the one with the ঘুঁটি; numbers are shown in Bangla digits.
    - **Feels BD:** the chalk-on-ground classic, still unbuilt on the site.
22. **ষোলোগুটি** · 7–12 · BD · M/L
    - **Builds:** planning and strategy.
    - **Loop:** the traditional 16-piece jumping board game against a gentle AI or pass-and-play.
    - **Feels BD:** a grandparents' game, drawn as chalk lines on a cemented উঠোন.

### 6.4 Nature, culture and the country (BD)

23. **ষড়ঋতুর চাকা** · 5–10 · BD · S/M
    - **Builds:** the six seasons, Bangla months and observation.
    - **Loop:** a wheel of six seasons; drag cards (কদম, কাশফুল, খেজুরের রস, কালবৈশাখী, নবান্ন, কোকিল) to their season, and the wheel turns colour.
24. **কার বাসা কোনটা?** · 4–8 · BD · S
    - **Builds:** animal and bird knowledge; habitats.
    - **Loop:** match the বাবুই to its hanging nest, the মাছরাঙা to the riverbank, the বাঘ to the সুন্দরবন, the ইলিশ to the river and sea, the চড়ুই to the ঘুলঘুলি.
25. **সুন্দরবনে নৌকাভ্রমণ** · 6–11 · BD · M
    - **Builds:** observation and conservation.
    - **Loop:** steer a small boat through the mangrove channels and spot animals (বাঘের পায়ের ছাপ, চিত্রা হরিণ, কুমির, মাছরাঙা, শুশুক) for a sticker book; one "keep the forest clean" choice per trip.
26. **বাংলাদেশের মানচিত্র জোড়া** · 8–12 · BD · M
    - **Builds:** geography (the বাংলাদেশ ও বিশ্বপরিচয় curriculum).
    - **Loop:** a jigsaw of the 8 divisions, then drag rivers (পদ্মা, মেঘনা, যমুনা) into place; each piece shows one local fruit or sight.
    - **Note:** different from *desh-cheno*, which covers countries.
27. **আমাদের উৎসব** · 5–10 · BD · S/M
    - **Builds:** respect for every faith and culture.
    - **Loop:** match festival cards to their things (ঈদ: চাঁদ, সেমাই, সালামি; দুর্গাপূজা: ঢাক, মেলা; বুদ্ধ পূর্ণিমা: ফানুস, প্রদীপ; বড়দিন: তারা, কেক; বৈসাবি: ফুল ভাসানো; পহেলা বৈশাখ: মুখোশ, পান্তা-ইলিশ). Each completed card says one line about sharing.
    - **Note:** no deities pictured.
28. **টুনটুনির গল্প** · 4–8 · BD · M
    - **Builds:** listening and reading, cause and effect, cleverness over strength.
    - **Loop:** an illustrated, tappable retelling of Upendrakishore's public-domain টুনটুনি stories, with small choices ("টুনটুনি এখন কী করবে?").
    - **Note:** pick the gentle stories.

### 6.5 Values and feelings (BD)

29. **মনের রং** · 4–8 · BD · S/M
    - **Builds:** emotion recognition and empathy (SEL).
    - **Loop:** scenes from BD childhood (ঈদে নতুন জামা, ঘুড়ি কেটে গেল, বন্ধু টিফিন ভাগ দিল, ছোট ভাই খাতা ছিঁড়ল). Name the feeling, then pick a kind response.
30. **কে কী কাজ করে** · 4–8 · BD · S
    - **Builds:** dignity of all work; community helpers.
    - **Loop:** match tools to people (কৃষক, জেলে, মাঝি, রিকশাচালক, পোশাককর্মী, ডাক্তার, শিক্ষক, দোকানি), then a short "thank you" card for each.
    - **Note:** women and men in any role.
31. **ঘরের কাজ ভাগ** · 6–10 · BD · S
    - **Builds:** fairness and gender equality (the Meena theme, not Meena).
    - **Loop:** a family's chores (পানি আনা, থালা ধোয়া, হাঁস-মুরগিকে খাবার, বিছানা গোছানো) are split among ভাই, বোন, আব্বু and আম্মু. The board shows when it's unfair, and everyone finishes faster when the load is fair.
32. **ছাদের পাখির পানি** · 4–9 · BD · S/M
    - **Builds:** kindness to animals and responsibility.
    - **Loop:** in the গ্রীষ্ম heat, set out water bowls and rice grains on the roof. Birds (চড়ুই, শালিক, কাক, ঘুঘু) come; return each "day" (localStorage, optional) to refill. A stray kitten gets a dry box in the বর্ষা.
33. **রান্নাবাটি** · 4–8 · BD · M
    - **Builds:** sequencing, healthy eating and pretend play.
    - **Loop:** follow a 4–5 step picture recipe (খিচুড়ি, ডিম ভাজি, ফলের সালাদ) by dragging ingredients in order; then build a balanced plate (ভাত, ডাল, সবজি, মাছ/ডিম, ফল).
    - **Note:** the child is never at the stove alone; a grown-up's hand appears for the চুলা steps.

### 6.6 Bringing the world to a Bangladeshi child (world / both)

34. **পৃথিবী ঘুরি** · 6–12 · both · M
    - **Builds:** geography, cultures, respect.
    - **Loop:** a passport stamp book. Fly from Dhaka to a country: learn its greeting (written in Bangla script, e.g. জাপানে "কোনিচিওয়া"), one food, one landmark and one festival, then answer one question to get the stamp.
    - **Feels BD:** ties back home ("জাপানেও ভাত খায়!") and to the countries where প্রবাসী relatives live.
    - **Note:** different from *desh-cheno*, which is a quiz.
35. **সৌরজগৎ সাজাও** · 6–12 · world · M
    - **Builds:** the planets, order, size and distance.
    - **Loop:** drag planets into orbit order, then by size; each fits with a fact in Bangla. A bonus round covers why the moon (চাঁদ মামা) changes shape.
36. **চাঁদের কলা** · 7–12 · both · S/M
    - **Builds:** moon phases and calendars.
    - **Loop:** spin the moon around the earth and match phases to names (অমাবস্যা, পূর্ণিমা) and to the festivals that follow the moon (ঈদের চাঁদ, বুদ্ধ পূর্ণিমা, লক্ষ্মী পূজা).
    - **Note:** science only, with no ruling on sighting.
37. **ডাইনোসরের হাড়** · 5–10 · world · M
    - **Builds:** fossils, the deep past, classification.
    - **Loop:** brush away soil to find bones, assemble the skeleton puzzle, then sort it as plant-eater or meat-eater with one fact each.
38. **সাগরের তলায়** · 6–12 · both · M
    - **Builds:** ocean zones and marine life.
    - **Loop:** sort creatures by depth (sunlit, twilight, dark).
    - **Feels BD:** includes the Bay of Bengal's own deep canyon, the Swatch of No Ground, with its dolphins and whales, and Cox's Bazar sea turtles.
39. **কে কোথায় থাকে, পৃথিবীজুড়ে** · 4–8 · world · S
    - **Builds:** habitats and adaptation.
    - **Loop:** drag animals to biomes (পেঙ্গুইন: বরফ, উট: মরুভূমি, ক্যাঙারু: অস্ট্রেলিয়ার মাঠ, পান্ডা: বাঁশবন, বাঘ: সুন্দরবন); "why" cards explain fur, humps and pouches.
40. **আবিষ্কারের সিঁড়ি** · 8–12 · both · M
    - **Builds:** history of science, sequencing, curiosity.
    - **Loop:** order invention cards on a timeline (চাকা, কাগজ, ছাপাখানা, বাল্ব, উড়োজাহাজ, মোবাইল) and meet the people behind them, from every culture: ইবনে আল-হাইসাম, মারি কুরি, রাইট ভাইয়েরা, **জগদীশচন্দ্র বসু**, **সত্যেন্দ্রনাথ বসু**, **ফজলুর রহমান খান**.
41. **বিশ্বের বিস্ময়** · 6–12 · both · S/M
    - **Builds:** world landmarks and spatial puzzle skills.
    - **Loop:** tile-slide or jigsaw puzzles of landmarks (পিরামিড, চীনের প্রাচীর, তাজমহল, আইফেল টাওয়ার), with BD's own UNESCO sites alongside them (ষাটগম্বুজ মসজিদ, পাহাড়পুর বিহার, সুন্দরবন).
    - **Note:** respectful treatment of religious buildings.
42. **ভাসে না ডোবে?** · 5–10 · both · M
    - **Builds:** the scientific method (predict, test, explain).
    - **Loop:** predict whether a নারকেল, ইট, শুকনো পাতা, স্টিলের চামচ, বোতল or কলার ভেলা floats, drop it in a simulated পুকুর, see the result and get one "why".
    - **Feels BD:** universal physics with BD objects.
43. **এখন ওখানে কটা বাজে?** · 8–12 · both · M
    - **Builds:** time zones, day and night, earth's rotation.
    - **Loop:** spin the globe; when it's night in Dhaka, what is ছোট মামা doing in Riyadh, Rome, Kuala Lumpur or London? Pick the right scene (sleeping, at work, eating breakfast).
    - **Feels BD:** the প্রবাসী family is a near-universal BD experience.

## 8. Cautions that matter most

1. **Safety content shows the safe action and never the harm.** Pair each safety game with a parent note. For under-5s, the drowning and burns message is for adults.
2. **Copyright:** use folk ছড়া and pre-1966 authors only. **No Nazrul or Jasimuddin text** (until 2037), no NCTB textbook text or pictures, and **no Meena or Sisimpur characters, names or look-alikes**.
3. **Every faith equally, rulings never.** Show symbols, not deities or prophets. Avoid pork and alcohol. No moon-sighting or prayer-time claims.
4. **Politics:** no leaders' portraits and nothing on 2024. Keep 1952 and 1971 to dates, places and symbols.
5. **Mute-first:** some families avoid music, and children play in shared rooms. Every game must work silently, and the anthem is never a game sound.
6. **Inclusion:** girls and boys in every role, no colourism, children with disabilities, hill and plains peoples as neighbours.
7. **Privacy:** nothing a child types (a parent's number, names in the ভরসার বৃত্ত) leaves the phone or gets saved without need.
