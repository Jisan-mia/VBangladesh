# Games wave 2: the next 25 (October 2026)

These come from the 123-idea research in `research/games-2026-10.md`. The owner asked for "the rest that aren't duplicates", leaning towards puzzles and brain games, with levels and rewards in each. The 25 below are the shortlist reserves (§4.4) plus the strongest puzzle ideas cut on score (§5), now that a shared sound, level and confetti kit exists (`research/game-engagement-brief.md`). IDs refer to §1 of the research file.

**Skipped on purpose:**
- দম ধরো and আগে ছোঁও: duplicate loops of existing games.
- বাসে সিট দখল: needs four fingers on one phone.
- আঙুল ক্রিকেট: Bangladeshi use is unverified.
- মৌচাক, the Wordle-style game and শব্দছক: need a large vetted Bangla dictionary, with messy conjuncts.
- কাকুরো: too hard to explain.
- জেলার মানচিত্র: needs map geometry for 64 districts.
- Card games: gambling stigma.

**Built as modes in the upgrade wave instead:** কাটাকুটি (in ছক-জোড়া), মার্বেল (in ক্যারম).

Each brief gives the hook, the core loop, the levels or progression, the sound identity, and things to watch. The agent designs the rest to the bar in `research/game-agent-prompt.md` and the engagement brief.

## Puzzle and brain (14)

#### 1. golokdhadha · গোলকধাঁধা (W01)
- **Hook:** the maze pages at the back of children's magazines, set in পুরান ঢাকার গলি. Get from home to the জিলাপির দোকান before it closes.
- **Loop:** drag or swipe a dot through a seeded maze. Arrow keys work too, and tapping a cell moves along a straight corridor.
- **Levels:** 30, from 5×5 to 15×15 in three worlds (পুরান ঢাকা, গ্রামের মেঠোপথ, সুন্দরবনের খাল). Stars come from time against a par computed from the shortest path length. A daily maze too.
- **Sound:** a soft footstep tick per cell, a bump at walls, and a shop bell at the exit.
- **Watch:** the generator is a seeded recursive backtracker, tested that the exit is reachable. Keep the line thick enough for a thumb.

#### 2. ponero-ghor · ১৫ ঘরের ধাঁধা (W02)
- **Hook:** the tin sliding-tile toy from the মেলা, with embossed Bangla digits.
- **Loop:** tap a tile next to the gap to slide it, or swipe.
- **Levels:** 3×3 (১–৮) for levels 1–10, 4×4 for 11–25, 5×5 for 26–30. Shuffles are made by random legal moves from solved, so they're always solvable. Stars come from moves against a par (for 3×3, compute the optimum with BFS in tests). There's also a "ছবির ধাঁধা" mode where the tiles form a simple drawn picture (নৌকা, রিকশা).
- **Sound:** a tin clink per slide and a rising chord when a row is complete.
- **Watch:** the parity check, and a tested solver for 3×3. Don't use the 15-puzzle's classic look (a wooden frame is fine).

#### 3. switchboard · সুইচবোর্ড (W14)
- **Hook:** the old wooden switchboard with a row of বাতি. Turn every bulb on. Load-shedding humour in the copy ("সব বাতি জ্বালাও, কারেন্ট থাকতে থাকতে").
- **Loop:** tapping a switch toggles its bulb and its neighbours (lights-out).
- **Levels:** 30, from 3×3 to 5×5. Each puzzle is generated by pressing k random switches from all-on, so it's solvable with par = k. Stars come from moves against par. A 20-second animated tutorial on level 1 only (just a pulsing hint on the switch to press).
- **Sound:** a crisp টক্‌ per switch, the bulb filament's tiny hum rising with how many are lit, and a fan-whirr chord when all are lit.
- **Watch:** an undo button, and a hint that shows one switch from the solution (computed by linear algebra over GF(2)).

#### 4. sudoku · সুডোকু বাংলায় (W04)
- **Hook:** the newspaper puzzle page, with Bangla digits ১–৯.
- **Loop:** tap a cell, then a digit from a big pad. Pencil marks (খসড়া), undo, mistakes shown gently, and a hint that fills one cell.
- **Levels:** four tiers. সহজ 4×4 for learners, মাঝারি 6×6, কঠিন 9×9 with fewer givens, ওস্তাদ 9×9. The generator guarantees a unique solution (tested), and the ladder is 40 levels plus a daily puzzle. Stars come from time and hints used.
- **Sound:** a soft pencil tick on entry, a chime when a row, column or box completes, and confetti when the whole grid is solved.
- **Watch:** generator speed on a cheap phone. Pre-generate level seeds offline into `lib/levels.ts` if generation takes more than 200 ms. The 9×9 pad must fit 320px.

#### 5. chhobi-photao · ছবি ফোটাও (W15)
- **Hook:** a nonogram where every solved grid reveals a Bangladeshi picture: ইলিশ, নৌকা, শাপলা, রিকশা, আম, ঘুড়ি, হারিকেন, কাঁঠাল, চায়ের কাপ, শহীদ মিনার, and so on.
- **Loop:** tap to fill, tap again to mark ✕. The row and column number clues dim when satisfied.
- **Levels:** 30, from 5×5 to 10×10. Level 1 is a 5×5 that teaches by doing. Every picture is hand-authored in `lib/pictures.ts`, and a test proves each has a unique line-solvable solution. An album of revealed pictures is the collection.
- **Sound:** a pencil scratch on fill, a soft tick on ✕, and on solve the picture colours in with an `sfx.win()`.
- **Watch:** a drag-to-fill row on touch, and a big enough grid at 320px (10×10 is the max).

#### 6. ghontar-sur · ঘণ্টার সুর (W09)
- **Hook:** four clay pots (মাটির কলসি) or temple-fair bells, each with its own note. Repeat the tune.
- **Loop:** watch and listen as the pots light and sound in sequence, then tap them back. The sequence grows by one.
- **Levels:** stages of 5 (৫, ১০, ১৫, ২০…). Speed rises per stage. A "রোজের সুর" daily seed. The best length is the score.
- **Sound:** `sfx.note()` mapped to four pentatonic pads. This is the game where sound matters most.
- **Watch:** it must still be playable muted (each pot flashes a big colour and number). Lights flash at most 3 times a second. Slow mode lengthens the gaps.

#### 7. konta-nei · কোনটা নেই? (W10)
- **Hook:** the মুদি দোকানের তাক. Look at the shelf, the shopkeeper turns away, one item vanishes, and you name it.
- **Loop:** see N items for a few seconds, then pick the missing one from the choices.
- **Levels:** 30. The item count grows (4 → 12), the viewing time shrinks, and later levels move two items or swap one. Stars come from a correct first try and speed.
- **Sound:** a shutter or "চোখ বন্ধ" whoosh, a cash-box ding when right, and a soft wrong.
- **Watch:** items are drawn simply with distinct silhouettes and colours (চাল, ডাল, তেল, লবণ, চা-পাতা, বিস্কুট, ডিম, পেঁয়াজ…), each with its name. Seeded.

#### 8. bhul-kothay · ভুল কোথায়? (W11)
- **Hook:** the "দুটো ছবির ৫টা অমিল" page from children's magazines.
- **Loop:** two copies of a bazaar or village scene. Tap the differences on either.
- **Levels:** 30. The scenes are composed in code from drawn parts (stalls, fruit, signboards, sky, boats), and the differences are chosen by seed: colour, missing item, flipped, size. Later levels have subtler differences and 7 instead of 5. Stars come from time and wrong taps.
- **Sound:** a pop and a red ring on each find, and a soft buzz on a wrong tap.
- **Watch:** the tap radius is generous. Every difference must be visible at 320px. A test proves each difference changes pixels in its region.

#### 9. atir-aam · আঁটি থেকে আম (C04)
- **Hook:** a 2048-style merge but a growing mango story: আঁটি → চারা → গাছ → মুকুল → গুটি আম → কাঁচা আম → পাকা আম → আমের ঝুড়ি → আমের বাগান.
- **Loop:** swipe the board. Two of the same merge into the next stage.
- **Levels or milestones:** each new stage reached is a milestone with a short celebration and card badge. Stars per run come from the highest stage. Optional goal levels ("পাকা আম বানাও ২০০ চালের মধ্যে").
- **Sound:** a plop on merge with rising pitch for combos (`sfx.combo`), and a bigger chord for a new stage.
- **Watch:** don't copy 2048's look, colours or numbering. The tiles are drawn, not numbers. Undo is one step only.

#### 10. bhimruler-chak · ভিমরুলের চাক (R20)
- **Hook:** the mango orchard has hidden hornet nests. Clear the bushes safely. The numbers tell how many nests are next to a bush.
- **Loop:** minesweeper. Tap to clear, long-press or use the "গামছা" mode toggle to mark a nest.
- **Levels:** 30, from 6×6 with 4 nests up to 10×14. The first tap is always safe and opens an area. Prefer a no-guess generator (tested by a solver), with retry until it's solvable. Stars come from time.
- **Sound:** a leaf rustle on clear, a soft buzz on a nest marker, and a buzzing swarm (`loop('hum')`, short) on a nest. It is funny, not scary.
- **Watch:** a 3-step interactive tutorial on level 1. Cartoon only, nobody gets hurt ("ভোঁওও… পালাও!").

#### 11. churir-bazar · চুড়ির বাজার (C03)
- **Hook:** the glass bangle stall at the মেলা. Match three bangles of a colour.
- **Loop:** swap neighbours to make lines of 3 or more. Match 4 for a sparkle bangle, 5 for a rainbow.
- **Levels:** 40 with goals ("২০টা লাল চুড়ি তোলো, ২৫ চালে"). Stars come from the score at the end.
- **Sound:** a glass clink chord per match, rising with cascades (`sfx.combo`).
- **Watch:** no candy or sweets imagery, nothing like the genre leader's look. Always have a valid move (reshuffle with a "চুড়ি ঝাঁকাও" animation). No paid boosters, ever.

#### 12. mukhe-mukhe-hisab · মুখে মুখে হিসাব (W26)
- **Hook:** the দোকানদার who adds your bill in his head faster than a calculator.
- **Loop:** sums appear as a bazaar list ("৩ হালি ডিম, হালি ৪৫ টাকা"). Type the answer on a big Bangla number pad, or pick one of four in kids mode.
- **Levels:** 30, progressing through addition, subtraction, multiplication, mixed and বাজারের হিসাব word problems. A 60-second round per level, with stars from correct answers. The combo streak raises points.
- **Sound:** a cash-register "ক্যাচিং" when right, rising combo notes, and a soft wrong.
- **Watch:** the numbers are seeded and tested, with no negative or fractional answers in the kids levels.

#### 13. chhobir-tukro · ছবির টুকরো (W12)
- **Hook:** a jigsaw of a village painting: a river, a boat, a sunset, a paddy field. The scene is generated in code from layers, seeded, so every level is a new painting.
- **Loop:** drag pieces to snap into place. Tapping a piece then a slot works too.
- **Levels:** 30, from 3×3 to 6×6 (later levels rotate pieces 90°). Stars come from time.
- **Sound:** a cardboard snap on a correct place and a soft slide on a wrong drop.
- **Watch:** keep drag performance smooth (draw pieces from an offscreen canvas). The snap tolerance must be generous at 320px.

#### 14. sat-chara · সাত চাড়া (D22)
- **Hook:** the seven flat stones (খোলামকুচি) stacked in the গলি. Knock them down with the ball.
- **Loop:** a one-phase physics puzzle. Drag back to aim and set power, release, and the ball hits the stack. Score comes from stones knocked.
- **Levels:** 30 arrangements (straight stack, a pyramid, behind a brick, on a step). Stars come from stones down per throw (3 throws per level).
- **Sound:** a ball thwack, a clatter of stones, and a dog barking far away on a miss (very soft, funny).
- **Watch:** use simple, stable physics (circles and boxes with a basic impulse solver, or scripted topple rules). It must not jitter. Test that the simulation is deterministic per seed.

## Arcade with progression (11)

#### 15. chhip-felo · ছিপ ফেলো (C12)
- **Hook:** pond fishing with a bamboo rod.
- **Loop:** cast, wait for the float (ফাতনা) to dip, and tap at the right moment. A short reel tug follows.
- **Progression:** a fish album (collection, 20 fish with Bangla names: পুঁটি, কই, শিং, রুই, বোয়াল…) and three places (পুকুর, বিল, নদী) that unlock by album count. There are rare fish.
- **Sound:** the plop of a cast, a ripple, a reel whirr, a splash, and a "ধরা পড়ল!" chord.
- **Watch:** the fish list is the game's own (don't import from other tools). No gore. A catch shows the fish proudly, then it goes in the basket.

#### 16. golir-badminton · গলির ব্যাডমিন্টন (R12)
- **Hook:** winter-night badminton in the গলি under a bulb, with the net tied between two windows.
- **Loop:** tap or hold to swing as the shuttle comes. Timing sets the return angle.
- **Progression:** a rally count against a bot that gets better through 5 opponents (পাশের বাসার ভাইয়া → পাড়ার চ্যাম্পিয়ন). Wind and fog nights arrive as stages.
- **Sound:** a racket ping, a shuttle swish, and a sigh from the crowd (a window) on a miss.
- **Watch:** keep the physics readable and slowed in slow mode.

#### 17. latim · লাটিম (D17)
- **Hook:** winding the string and throwing the wooden top.
- **Loop:** swipe down to throw (the swipe's speed and straightness give spin and wobble), then tilt-free taps to "nudge" the wobble. The score is spin time.
- **Progression:** surfaces as stages (মাটির উঠান, পাকা মেঝে, টিনের চাল), and tricks (pick up the top on your palm by tapping when it slows: a bonus).
- **Sound:** a whirr that falls in pitch as the spin slows (`loop('hum')` pitch and volume), and a clack when it falls.
- **Watch:** the swipe must feel good on cheap touchscreens. Show the throw quality visually.

#### 18. kagojer-urojahaj · কাগজের উড়োজাহাজ (R02)
- **Hook:** the paper plane from the back page of your exercise book flies through a monsoon sky. Non-violent: you pop rain clouds and collect sunshine.
- **Loop:** drag to steer. It fires paper balls automatically, and dark clouds take 2 hits and burst into rain.
- **Levels:** 20 waves with new cloud patterns. Every 5th wave has a big "কালবৈশাখী" storm cloud. Stars come from clouds cleared and no hits.
- **Sound:** paper swishes, a soft pop per cloud, a thunder rumble for the storm, and birdsong on a cleared wave.
- **Watch:** no guns, enemies or explosions. Not a copy of any classic shooter look.

#### 19. nodi-par · নদী পার (R11)
- **Hook:** crossing the river by hopping on নৌকা, ভেলা and কচুরিপানা to reach the other ghat.
- **Loop:** tap to hop forward, swipe sideways. Falling in is a splash and a restart (no death imagery).
- **Levels:** 30 rivers, with faster currents, a ferry wake, and rain. Stars come from time and the mangoes collected.
- **Sound:** a hop, a splash, the ferry's horn, and the ghat's bell on arrival.
- **Watch:** not the look of any famous lane-hopping game. The theme and art are our own.

#### 20. marble-sinri · মার্বেল-সিঁড়ি (R05)
- **Hook:** a marble rolling down endless ledges (the old phone game everyone remembers, re-skinned as a marble on stair steps).
- **Loop:** tap left or right, or tilt with arrows. Don't touch the top. Avoid thorny (cactus-pot) ledges and collect মার্বেল.
- **Progression:** the speed rises in stages, with milestones on the card. The skin unlocks marble colours by milestones (a collection).
- **Sound:** a roll rumble, a click on landing, and a glassy ting on pickup.
- **Watch:** it must be fair: spawned gaps are always reachable (tested).

#### 21. penalty · পেনাল্টি কিক (C15)
- **Hook:** a para tournament shootout.
- **Loop:** swipe the ball, where the direction and curve come from the swipe path. In the keeper turn, tap a side to dive.
- **Levels:** a tournament of 8 opponents (পাড়া → থানা → জেলা → জাতীয়) with keeper skill rising. Each match is a best-of-5 shootout. Stars per match.
- **Sound:** a kick thump, the net swish, a crowd "ওওও" or cheer (synthesized noise swell), and a whistle.
- **Watch:** no real club or team names or kits. Sudden death on a tie.

#### 22. roshi-tana · রশি টানাটানি (D35)
- **Hook:** the sports-day tug-of-war.
- **Loop:** the pull comes from rhythm, not mashing: tap on the beat of the "হেঁইয়ো" for strong pulls.
- **Modes:** two players on one phone (each half of the screen), or a solo ladder of 10 bot teams.
- **Sound:** the "হেঁইয়ো" rhythm beat, rope creak, and a cheer for the winner.
- **Watch:** the beat timing works on cheap phones (judge by the tap time against the beat clock, not frame-perfect). Each player's zone is ≥ 45% of the screen.

#### 23. moshar-raat · মশার রাত (C06)
- **Hook:** the electric mosquito bat at night, with the light switched off.
- **Loop:** tap mosquitoes as they zip in. Each one zapped is a spark and a "চট!".
- **Levels:** 20 nights. Mosquitoes get faster and come in swarms. Some nights have a মশারি to protect. Stars come from mosquitoes zapped and the বালিশ protected.
- **Sound:** the mosquito whine (`loop`, volume follows the nearest one), a bat crackle on a hit.
- **Watch:** dengue is serious. Include exactly one calm line, the official tip "জমা পানি ফেলে দাও" in the FAQ, with no fear copy. Cartoon mosquitoes, no gore.

#### 24. al-dhore-hati · আল ধরে হাঁটি (C20)
- **Hook:** walking along the narrow আল between paddy fields without slipping into the mud.
- **Loop:** hold and drag left or right to keep your feet on the winding ridge. Tapping the left or right halves works as an alternative.
- **Levels:** 20 fields that grow more winding, with a puddle, a goat on the path or wind. Stars come from distance without a slip.
- **Sound:** soft footsteps, a squelch on a slip, and birds.
- **Watch:** the brief noted steady dragging is hard, so the tap alternative must be fully playable and slow mode gentle.

#### 25. o-te-ojogor · অ-তে অজগর (W23)
- **Hook:** the বর্ণ পরিচয় book. Letters fall like ripe fruit, and you catch them in order with a basket.
- **Loop:** drag or tap-left/right to move the basket and catch the right next letter. A wrong letter makes the basket wobble.
- **Levels:** স্বরবর্ণ (অ–ঔ), ব্যঞ্জনবর্ণ in groups, then short words (আম, জল, ফুল…), then numbers ১–২০. Stars per level. Kid-friendly first, but fun for adults on the fast levels.
- **Sound:** a letter-catch pop, and the matching picture word shown ("অ-তে অজগর") with a chime.
- **Watch:** use the site font, with the letters big and clear. The sequences are data in `lib/` with tests. Nothing frightening (the অজগর is a friendly drawing).
