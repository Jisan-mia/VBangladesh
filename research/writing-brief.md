# Writing pass: clear, crisp, quietly warm

The owner's note, in short: people come to learn something, get an answer, or have fun. Decoration, exaggeration and over-explaining make the site feel less valuable. Write the way good Bangla prose is written: short, concrete, confident. This pass builds on `research/bangla-voice-guide.md` and `research/bangla-copy-review.md`, and those rules still hold.

## The three tests for every line
1. **Does it help the person do or understand something?** If not, cut it.
2. **Is it true and plain?** No inflated feeling, no metaphor stacked on the tool's own metaphor, no promise the tool doesn't keep.
3. **Would a thoughtful Bangladeshi writer write it this way?** Read it out loud. If it sounds like an ad, a translation or a greeting card, rewrite it.

## How good Bangla prose works (style notes, no copying)
- **হুমায়ূন আহমেদ:** very short sentences. Everyday words. Feeling comes from one small, exact detail, never from adjectives. Lots of white space.
- **সৈয়দ মুজতবা আলী:** wit that's light and dry. The joke is in the observation, not in exclamation marks.
- **রবীন্দ্রনাথের প্রবন্ধ / শিশুসাহিত্য (সহজ পাঠ):** plain words that a child understands, a clear rhythm, no ornament for its own sake.
- **Taken together:** say the thing. One idea per sentence. Concrete nouns (হারিকেন, ঠোঙা, টিফিন বাটি) beat abstract ones (স্মৃতি, অনুভূতি, আনন্দ). Trust the reader.

## Cut these
- **Decorative lines that add nothing to the tool's use.**
  - tokhon-ekhon: "একটা সাল বেছে নাও, আব্বুর পুরোনো মানিব্যাগ খুলে দেখো…" → "একটা সাল আর টাকার অঙ্ক বেছে নাও, দেখো আজকের হিসাবে সেটা কত।"
  - Human lines like "দাম বেড়েছে পাঁচ গুণ, তোমার গল্প তার চেয়েও বেশি।" become a plain, useful line ("১৯৯৫-এর ১০০ টাকায় যা কেনা যেত, আজ তা কিনতে লাগে প্রায় ৬৭০ টাকা।"), or are dropped.
  - The tool's visual metaphor (wallet, envelope, slate) can stay in the art; the words don't need to keep narrating it.
- **Stating the obvious, which cheapens the thing.**
  - salami-chek "খেলনা চেক · শুধু মজার জন্য": everyone knows a salami cheque from an app is play. Remove the repeated "খেলনা" and "শুধু মজা" labels.
  - The same goes for "শুধু মজা, আর কিছু না" style lines in quizzes (biye-kobe, moner-boyos, flames, bhorta, adda…). Remove them where the fun is obvious.
- **Exaggeration:** "অসাধারণ", "দারুণ সব", "জীবন বদলে", "সেরা অভিজ্ঞতা", stacked "!", forced rhymes, emotional claims the tool can't back.
- **Over-explaining:** steps that narrate what the screen already shows, and repeated privacy reassurances. Say it once, plainly, in the privacy text.
- **Filler openers:** "এখানে তুমি…", "এই টুলটি দিয়ে…", "চলো দেখি…".

## Keep these (they protect people, so they aren't over-explaining)
- **Honest labels:** "আনুমানিক" on estimates, "সম্ভাব্য" on moon-dependent dates, sources and "যাচাই" dates, and safety lines (blood bank checks you; kites away from wires).
- **One quiet note where a real misunderstanding could cause harm:** a "prediction" game (biye-kobe) keeps one short FAQ answer that it isn't a real prediction. Keep it calm, never a banner.
- **Accurate privacy promises.** Simplify the words, never the meaning.
- **SEO keywords** in titles and H1 kickers (just added): keep the keyword and polish the wording only.

## Shape of a good tool page
- **Lead (1–2 sentences):** what you put in, what you get. Example: "জন্মতারিখ দাও, বয়স দেখো বছর-মাস-দিনে।"
- **Steps (2–4):** one action each. Skip any step the screen makes obvious.
- **Human result lines:** one honest observation, sometimes a light, dry smile. Never a lecture, never a greeting card.
- **FAQ:** answer in the first sentence, then stop.

## What must not change
Facts, numbers, sources, dates, ids, link and hash formats, tally ids, append-only data order, test-pinned logic, and the SEO title, description and H1 limits (unique site-wide). Only visible wording changes.
