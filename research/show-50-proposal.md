# Which 50 tools to show (proposal, not implemented)

The idea: add a `hidden` flag to tools in `lib/catalog.ts`. Hidden tools vanish from the homepage, `/tools`, "related tools" and the sitemap. Their pages still open by direct link, so links already shared never break. Turning one back on is a one-word change. **Nothing is built until the owner says so.**

How the 50 were chosen:
- all 10 launch heroes from the marketing plan;
- strong share loops and real community homes (from the post copywriters' fit notes);
- every category represented;
- utility tools with search demand.

The 36 hidden tools are seasonal (switch them on in season), waiting for a human review, or weaker or overlapping.
