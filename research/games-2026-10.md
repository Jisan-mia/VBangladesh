# গেম: a retro-games category for একটুখানি (October 2026)

Prepared 11 October 2026. Research only: no code was written and nothing else in the repo was changed.

**What this is.** A funnel of 123 ideas → 50 → 25 for a new **গেম** category: retro, evergreen games that feel familiar to Bangladeshis, playable in the browser in seconds, in the standard of `research/tool-design-brief.md` (instant start, a card worth posting, a link that reopens it, তুমি voice).

**The short answer.**

- The 25 are listed in §4, with a mini-brief for each. Split: 7 retro digital, 7 desi board, paper and playground, 6 casual re-skins, 5 word and brain.
- **Build first (wave 1):** কালবৈশাখী আম, দুধ উথলানোর আগে, ঝালমুড়ি সাপ, ডাংগুলি, অ্যান্টেনা ঠিক করো. All small, all one loop, and together they show the whole category (§4.2).
- **Riskiest areas:** পাটি বুনি (falling blocks: the strongest IP exposure), carrom and Ludo-family games (the "is it permissible?" searches and gambling stigma), cards (left out), মশার রাত (dengue), and anything that touches police, banknotes or "গুলি" wording (§6).
- **Evidence caveat:** I found Bangladeshi sources for brick games, arcades, consoles, cyber cafés and the desi folk games. I found none for Nokia Snake, minesweeper or solitaire in Bangladesh, and none for TV antenna memories. Those rows are marked `(u)` and should be checked with the 90s-kids groups before the nostalgia scores are trusted (§7).

## 0. How to read this file

- **`[S12]`** points to §8. **`(u)`** means unsourced: my own knowledge of the genre, or a near-universal memory, to be treated as a hypothesis. **`(u for Bangladesh)`** means the genre is documented elsewhere but I found no Bangladeshi source.
- **Effort:** S is about one slot of a typical tool in `research/build-queue.md` (one screen, one loop, the card, the link and the tests). M is about two to three slots. L is four or more. These are relative guesses, not estimates from code.
- **Links** follow the existing tools: each game is its own route and the hash carries the state. For games it is `#s=<seed>&t=<score to beat>&n=<first name>` plus game-specific fields.
- **Existing tools** I checked against: `cholo-kheli` (rules for eleven games, Ludo among them with a scoreboard), `ganer-koli` (scorekeeper with a letter draw), `shobdo-khela` (daily word pieces), `bangla-typing`, `mishron`, `dhadha`, `namta`, `flames`, `banan`, `bagdhara`, and for theme collisions `tong`, `cassette`, `current-gelo`, `ghuri`, `chithi`, `mama-jaben`, `riksharong`, `mache-bhate`, `jam`, `adda`, `namer-design`.
- Skins I deliberately avoided because a live tool already owns the object: the khata (আমার ছোটবেলা only), the slate (নামতা), the bench carving (ফ্লেমস), the কুপি lamp (ধাঁধা), the harmonium lid (গানের কলি), the clay pot (দেশি মিশ্রণ), the typewriter (টাইপিং), the cricket placard (শেষ বলে ছয়), the rickshaw (three tools).

Ideas the brief asked about that I judged weak are kept in the list so the reason for dropping them is on record (§5).

## 2. Scoring

### 2.1 The six scores and the weights

Every idea is scored 1–5 on six criteria. **E and I are inverse: 5 means very little effort and no risk.**

| Code | Criterion | 5 means | 1 means | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| N | Nostalgia and recognition for Bangladeshis | A sourced Bangladeshi memory, or a near-universal daily-life image | Unfamiliar | **×3** |
| R | Evergreen replay value | Plays well a hundred times (pure skill, endless, or daily) | Once is enough | **×2** |
| S | Simplicity: learn in five seconds | No text needed | Needs a tutorial | **×2** |
| C | Shareability and challenge loop | One number to beat, same seed, instantly comparable | Nothing to compare | **×3** |
| E | Build effort (inverse) | Hours to days: no physics, AI or authored content | L or more: AI, physics, content or a server | **×2** |
| I | IP and safety risk (inverse) | No IP, gambling, violence or sensitivity issue | Unacceptable | **×2** |

**Total = 3N + 2R + 2S + 3C + 2E + 2I, out of 70.**

**Why these weights.** The product grows by people recognising something and sending it on. So nostalgia and the share loop count 3 each, and the other four count 2. I did not give risk a higher weight, because risk is handled by gates (below), not by trading it against fun.

**How honest the scores are.** They are my judgement, not measurements. A difference of one or two points is noise. N is capped at 3 unless there is a Bangladeshi source or the memory is near-universal; where I gave 4 or 5 without a source, the row says `(u)`. I scored I as the risk that is left *after* the re-skin described in the row. This is also why the final 25 is not simply the top 25 by score (§4.1).

### 2.2 Gates

Any one of these removes an idea whatever its score:

- **OV**: overlaps a live tool. Merge it into that tool as a feature, or drop it.
- **MG**: merged into another new idea.
- **IP**: even after a re-skin it still sits close to a trademarked look, character, art or music.
- **GA**: gambling or gambling-adjacent: bets, stakes, prizes for coins, loot boxes, cards played for money.
- **VI**: violence beyond cartoon.
- **EF**: too complex or content-heavy for what it gives.
- **DA**: needs a server or a large dataset (the product has none, and promises no tracking).
- Soft flags: **TH** (the theme is close to a live tool but the loop differs), **LS** (lost on score), **DUP** (scored well but duplicated a loop already on the shortlist), **COV** (a coverage pick, see §3).

## 4. The final 25

### 4.1 How the 25 were chosen from the 50

1. **Quotas by sub-type:** 7 retro digital, 7 desi board, paper and playground, 6 casual re-skins, 5 word and brain. The owner asked for balance, and these counts follow where the strong ideas are.
2. **No two finals are re-skins of each other.** There is one lane game, one runner, one flappy-style game, one stacker, one hold-and-release game and one sliding puzzle. The closest relatives are the three one-tap timers (ডাংগুলি, দুধ উথলানোর আগে, বস্তার পাহাড়), which differ in what the tap measures: distance, closeness to a limit, alignment. The runner-up of a shared loop became a reserve (for example ছিপ ফেলো and গলির ব্যাডমিন্টন).
3. **A number to beat, or a natural two-on-one-phone game.** Almost all of the 25 give the challenge link something exact to compare. Six work for two or more people on one phone.
4. **Judgement over rank, in the open.** The 25 are not the top 25 by score. The lowest-scoring finals are বাঘবন্দি (51), শব্দ খোঁজা (54), ভো-কাট্টা (56), জ্যাম ছাড়াও (56), পাটি বুনি (57), ক্যারম (57) and ছক-জোড়া (57). They were kept for the quotas, for loop variety, or because the brief named them. Several reserves outscore them (the best are 63). The reason each reserve was passed over is in §4.4.

Coverage of the brief's named families:

- **In the 25:** snake, falling blocks, brick racing, top-down racing (as a boat race), duck-shoot and whack-a-mole (as চড়ুই তাড়াও), breakout, snakes and ladders, carrom, বাঘবন্দি, dots and boxes, the chit game, kites, ডাংগুলি, catch-the-falling-thing, endless runner, flappy, stacking, timing, hold-and-release, code-breaking, change-making, sliding puzzle, word search, memory pairs, rhythm (inside the boat race).
- **Not in the 25:** space shooter, bounce-ball, tank, platformer, run-and-gun, bomb-maze, minesweeper, solitaire, pinball (reserves or cut, §5), Ludo (overlaps চলো খেলি), ষোলো গুটি, কাটাকুটি (folded into ছক-জোড়া), নাম-দেশ-ফুল-ফল, অন্তাক্ষরী, ধাঁধা, নামতা, spelling bee (all overlaps, merged into the existing tools), card games (gambling and bots), match-3, 2048 (reserves), jigsaw, crossword, sudoku (cut).

**Build order.**

- **Wave 1: the first five, all S.** কালবৈশাখী আম, দুধ উথলানোর আগে, ঝালমুড়ি সাপ, ডাংগুলি, অ্যান্টেনা ঠিক করো.
  - *Why these.* They are the five with the best mix of score, recognition, one-loop simplicity and a clean challenge number. Together they cover three sub-types and need one shared shell (a seeded random generator, the card, the link, the reduced-motion toggle), which the other twenty reuse.
  - *Where they would be posted* (per `research/marketing/brief.md`, one tool per community): the snake and the antenna suit 90s-kids groups; ডাংগুলি suits desi-games nostalgia groups; the mango game suits Boishakh to Jyoishtho; the milk game suits any general group.
  - *Why not simply the top five by score.* স্যার ঘুরলেই থামো (66) and দেরি হয়ে গেল! (65) score slightly higher than ডাংগুলি and অ্যান্টেনা (64). Taking them would make the first wave three casual games. They go in at the head of wave 2.

- **Wave 3: M and L.** সাপলুডু, বাঘবন্দি, বাইচের নৌকা, ভো-কাট্টা, ভাংতি নাই!, জ্যাম ছাড়াও, শব্দ খোঁজা, ক্যারম (L), and পাটি বুনি last, after the owner has decided the IP question in its brief.

### 4.3 Mini-briefs

Each brief has: the concept with its original name and skin, the first screen, the core loop and controls, the result-card line, the link and challenge, the risks (IP, accessibility, other), the effort, and the existing tool it must not duplicate. Card lines are in তুমি voice and Bangla digits, as in `research/bangla-voice-guide.md`. Every card ends with a footer question, as rule 4 of the tool brief requires.

#### Retro digital (7)

**1. ঝালমুড়ি সাপ** · `jhalmuri-sap` · retro · **S**

- **Concept and skin.** The Nokia-phone snake. A cartoon snake raids a ঝালমুড়ির ঠোঙা: the food is মুড়ি, চানাচুর, পেঁয়াজ, কাঁচা মরিচ and তেঁতুল, and the arena is fenced with টিনের বেড়া.
- **First screen.** A big-cell 12×16 board. A three-cell snake is already crawling slowly. A faint swipe hint fades on the first touch. No menu.
- **Loop and controls.** Swipe anywhere to turn, or tap the left or right half to turn left or right of where the snake is heading (one thumb). Eat to grow (10 points; a golden chilli is 30). Speed rises every five items. A wall or the snake itself ends it. "আরেকবার" restarts in under a second.
- **Result card.** "ঝালমুড়ি সাপ · রাফি · ১২৪০". Human line by score: under 300, "ঠোঙা তো সবে খুলল!"; 300 to 900, "ঝাল সামলে ভালোই খেয়েছ।"; above 900, "ঠোঙা প্রায় খালি!". Footer: "তুমি কত পারবে?"
- **Link and challenge.** `#s=48213&t=1240&n=রাফি`. The seed fixes the order of the food, so a friend plays the same ঠোঙা. Banner: "রাফি ১২৪০ করেছে। একই ঠোঙা, তোমার পালা।"
- **Risks.**
  - *IP:* do not use "Snake", "Snake II" or "Xenzia", the Nokia name, logo or phone silhouette, Nokia's pixel font, ringtone or LCD colours. The mechanic itself is old and free (the 1976 arcade game Blockade [S16]).
  - *Accessibility:* tap-turn as an alternative to swiping; a "ধীরে" speed cap; no screen shake; food told apart by shape as well as colour.
  - *Other:* keep a plain cartoon snake. Some communities revere snakes, so no deity or ritual jokes.
- **Must not duplicate:** nothing live.

**2. হাঁড়িভাঙা** · `handi-bhanga` · retro · **S**

- **Concept and skin.** Breakout. Rows of clay pots hang at a fair stall; the ball is a cloth ball and the paddle is a লাঠি. হাঁড়িভাঙা is a real folk game (usually blindfolded) [S12]; here nobody is blindfolded.
- **First screen.** Five rows of seven pots, the ball resting on the stick, one line: "আঙুল সরাও, হাঁড়ি ভাঙো".
- **Loop and controls.** Drag sideways anywhere; the stick follows with an offset so the thumb never covers it. Tap to launch. Cracked pots take two hits. Three চান্স. Score is 10 per pot plus 50 per chance left.
- **Result card.** "হাঁড়িভাঙা · রাফি · ৩৫টার ৩৫টা, ২ চান্স বাকি". Footer: "তুমি সব ভাঙতে পারবে?"
- **Link and challenge.** The seed picks which pots are cracked; `#s=..&t=<score>&n=..`.
- **Risks.**
  - *IP:* no "Breakout" or "Arkanoid" names, no rainbow bricks, no lettered power-up capsules, no "Vaus"-style paddle.
  - *Accessibility:* offset drag, a slow-ball toggle, no particles under reduced motion.
- **Must not duplicate:** nothing live.

**3. পাটি বুনি** · `pati-buni` · retro · **M** · *owner decision on IP needed before building*

- **Concept and skin.** The brick game's signature mechanic [S31]; the "9999-in-1" on the box was marketing, and real units held only a handful of games [S31]. You weave a শীতলপাটি, a traditional woven mat that is especially associated with Sylhet (u): falling cane bundles slot into rows, and a finished row is woven and rolled away. I rejected an ইট skin: brick kilns are tied to child-labour and pollution stories.
- **First screen.** A half-woven mat with one piece already falling slowly, and four large buttons at the bottom: left, rotate, right, drop.
- **Loop and controls.** Slide, rotate, drop. A full row clears. Speed rises every ten rows. The stack reaching the top ends it. Score by rows, with a bonus for several at once.
- **Result card.** The woven pattern itself: "পাটি বুনি · রাফি · ১৮ সারি". Footer: "তুমি কয় সারি বুনবে?"
- **Link and challenge.** The seed fixes the order of the pieces, so a friend gets the same bag. `#s=..&t=18&n=..`.
- **Risks.**
  - *IP (the highest of the 25):* a US court found that a clone of Tetris copied the game's particular look and behaviour even though rules and mechanics are free [S19]. So do not use the seven tetrominoes as a set, a 10×20 well, a ghost piece, the next-piece box, the familiar colours, any "-tris" name or the well-known tune's arrangement. Use a custom set of 2- to 4-cell pieces that includes shapes that are not tetrominoes, an 8×14 well and no ghost piece. This is not legal advice. If the owner is uneasy, drop this game.
  - *Accessibility:* soft drop by a button, not a swipe; a drop-speed cap with a "ধীরে" toggle; no flashing on a clear (WCAG 2.3.1).
  - *Other:* credit the craft honestly and claim no partnership.
- **Must not duplicate:** nothing live.

  - *Accessibility:* a hold gesture is hard for some players, so add a "ছুঁয়ে চালাও, ছুঁয়ে থামাও" toggle (WCAG 2.5.1); cues are visual and haptic.
  - *Other:* the teacher is shown kindly, no cane and no punishment. The chit says something like "আজ টিফিনে ঝালমুড়ি?"; never an exam-cheating setting.
- **Must not duplicate:** ফ্লেমস uses a bench; this uses a row of benches and a blackboard. চোর-ডাকাত-পুলিশ-বাবু uses chits for roles; this uses a note you pass, so the object is different.

#### Word and brain (5)

**21. পাকা-কাঁচা** · `paka-kancha` · word and brain · **S**

- **Concept and skin.** Guess a secret four-digit code in seven tries. Each digit shows পাকা (right digit, right place), আধপাকা (right digit, wrong place) or কাঁচা (not in the code), as three mango icons.
- **First screen.** Four empty slots, a Bangla number pad, and one example row already shown with its feedback ("এভাবে বুঝবে"). It is never empty.
- **Loop and controls.** Type four digits, read the feedback, repeat. There is a daily code (by the Asia/Dhaka date) and "বন্ধুর জন্য কোড বানাও".
- **Result card.** A spoiler-free grid of mango icons: "পাকা-কাঁচা · আজকের কোড · ৪/৭". Footer: "তুমি কয়টায় পারবে?"
- **Link and challenge.** Daily: `#d=<date>&r=<feedback rows>` with no digits. A friend's code: `#c=<code>`, which is not secret, and the page says so.
- **Risks.**
  - *IP:* no "Mastermind" name or pegs; do not copy the name or exact green-yellow-grey grid of the word-guessing craze. Use mango icons and shapes.
  - *Accessibility:* the three states differ by shape and label, not colour alone; a big number pad.
  - *Other:* do not reuse শব্দ সাজাই's share-grid look.
- **Must not duplicate:** শব্দ সাজাই and আজকের ধাঁধা share the "one a day" habit but not the loop (numbers, not words or riddles).

**22. ভাংতি নাই!** · `bhangti-nai` · word and brain · **M**

- **Concept and skin.** You run a corner shop. A customer hands over a big note: "২০০ টাকা দিলাম, জিনিস ৮৭ টাকার". Tap chits (১, ২, ৫, ১০, ২০, ৫০, ১০০) to return the exact change within ten seconds.
- **First screen.** The counter, the customer's bubble, the chits tray, and one example already solved.
- **Loop and controls.** Ten customers. Each correct answer scores; faster gives a bonus. A wrong sum gets a playful "আরে, ১০ টাকা কম দিলে!", never a punishment.
- **Result card.** "ভাংতি নাই! · রাফি · ১০ জনের ৯ জনকে ঠিক ভাংতি". Footer: "তুমি কয়জনকে পারবে?"
- **Link and challenge.** The seed fixes the ten customers.
- **Risks.**
  - *IP and politics:* no real taka note art. Notes carry portraits and designs that are state-printed and politically charged, and they can change. Use plain coloured chits with numerals.
  - *Accessibility:* chits of at least 56 px; a "সময় নিয়ে" toggle removes the timer; Bangla numerals throughout.
  - *Other:* the amounts are made up, not real prices.
- **Must not duplicate:** মামা, যাবেন? (haggling), নামতা চ্যালেঞ্জ (drill) and চায়ের কাপে হিসাব (calculator) all use money or sums, but none is about making exact change.

**23. জ্যাম ছাড়াও** · `jam-chharao` · word and brain · **M**

- **Concept and skin.** A jammed Dhaka street on a grid. Slide CNGs, rickshaws, buses and trucks along their lanes to free your green CNG.
- **First screen.** Level 1 (solvable in three moves) with the exit marked, and "ছোঁও আর সরাও".
- **Loop and controls.** Drag a vehicle, or tap it and then an arrow. Vehicles move only along their length. 40 graded levels, generated and checked by a solver at build time. Moves are counted and the best known is shown.
- **Result card.** "জ্যাম ছাড়াও · রাফি · লেভেল ১২ — ৯ চালে (সেরা ৭ চাল)". Footer: "তুমি কত চালে পারবে?"
- **Link and challenge.** `#l=12&m=<moves>&n=..`, re-checked on open because it is deterministic. The challenge is the same level in fewer moves.
- **Risks.**
  - *IP:* the commercial traffic-jam puzzle has a trademarked name and a known card set. Make original levels, use a different grid (6×7), a different vehicle set and a green vehicle, not a red car at a right-edge exit.
  - *Accessibility:* tap-select plus arrow buttons as an alternative to dragging; patterns on the vehicles as well as colours.
  - *Other:* no ambulance stuck in the jam.
- **Must not duplicate:** জ্যামে জীবন is a time calculator; the theme is close, the loop is not.

**24. শব্দ খোঁজা** · `shobdo-khoja` · word and brain · **M**

- **Concept and skin.** A letter grid with six hidden words from a theme pack (মাছ, পিঠা, ফুল, ঋতু, খেলা). Swipe along cells; a found word is struck through with a pen line.
- **First screen.** The "ফুল" grid with one word already found as a demo.
- **Loop and controls.** Swipe, or tap the first cell and then the last. Six words. A timer counts up. A hint reveals a first cell.
- **Result card.** "শব্দ খোঁজা · ফুল · ৬টার ৬টা, ২:১৫". Footer: "তুমি কত সময়ে পারবে?"
- **Link and challenge.** The pack and the seed (the grid is generated from the seed and the solution is known); `t` is the time.
- **Risks.**
  - *IP:* do not scrape word lists from copyrighted dictionaries. Write the lists and check spellings against Bangla Academy usage.
  - *Accessibility:* tap-first and tap-last as an alternative to swiping; cells of at least 48 px.
  - *Bangla text:* each cell holds a whole letter cluster (for example "ক্ষ" or "কা"), so words must split cleanly into clusters. Test shaping on a cheap Android.
- **Must not duplicate:** শব্দ সাজাই (word pieces, one a day), শুদ্ধ বানান কোনটি? and বাগধারার ছবি. This is search-in-a-grid with packs and unlimited play. Keep it from becoming another daily word.

**25. জোড়া মেলাও** · `jora-melao` · word and brain · **S**

- **Concept and skin.** Memory pairs with fair-stall toys: বাঁশি, লাটিম, বেলুন, ঘুড়ি and others. 4×4 face-down tiles. Two players alternate on one phone, or play solo for fewest moves.
- **First screen.** The grid face-down with two tiles pre-flipped as a demo, and "দুটো মিললে থাকবে".
- **Loop and controls.** Flip two tiles; a match stays. Pairs are tracked per player. Solo counts moves.
- **Result card.** Solo: "জোড়া মেলাও · রাফি · ২৩ চালে". Two players: "রাফি ৫ — মিম ৩". Footer: "তুমি কত চালে পারবে?"
- **Link and challenge.** The seed fixes the layout.
- **Risks.**
  - *IP:* no cartoon characters on the tiles.
  - *Accessibility:* shape plus colour on tiles; a "৩×৪ সহজ" mode; instant flips under reduced motion.
  - *Other:* memory games can frustrate, so show the move count softly.
- **Must not duplicate:** nothing live.

**A note on the card games.** টুয়েন্টি নাইন and কল ব্রিজ are real household games in Bangladesh (Pagat says Call Bridge is popular in Bangladesh, India and Nepal [S18]), but each needs three bots with bidding logic, and playing cards carry a gambling stigma in many homes. If they are ever built: no stakes, no money words, a seeded deal as the challenge (every friend gets the same hands, which is a genuinely fair comparison), and the same "never answer a ruling" rule.

**A note on the name শব্দজব্দ.** The brief lists it as a paper game. The name is already used for a school word-quiz competition run by a Bengali newspaper site [S24], and Bangla Wordle-style clones already exist [S24], so I did not use the name for any of the 25.

## 6. Rules that apply to every game

### 6.1 Challenge links

- The hash carries a seed `s` (a 32-bit integer; use an integer-only random generator so every phone produces the same sequence), a target `t` (score, time or moves), a first name `n` (normalised, at most 20 characters), and game-specific fields such as `l`, `m` or `p`.
- Each game gets a `share.ts` like the existing tools: it normalises, caps every field, rejects hostile input and builds a personal preview ("রাফি ১২৪০ করেছে — তুমি পারবে?").
- **Be honest about verification.** A real-time score cannot be checked, so the page says "নিজের ফোনে খেলা". The deterministic games (বাঘবন্দি, ছক-জোড়া, জ্যাম ছাড়াও, পাকা-কাঁচা) re-check the moves when the link opens.
- No leaderboards and no stored scores. At most an opt-in tally of fixed buckets with a minimum sample, as the tool brief already allows.

### 6.2 Gambling, ethics and sensitivity

- **No stakes, odds, bets, prizes, paid coins, loot boxes, lucky draws or "টাকা জিতো" copy, anywhere.** Scores are points, stars, metres, seconds. Any token is free and never implies money.
- Never answer "is this game permitted?". Ludo, carrom and cards all trigger those searches [S27].
- No politics: no party colours, no hartal or election jokes. Police appear only as a chit role. No banknote designs.
- Respect faith: no snake-deity jokes, nothing about prayer, Eid or Puja except as friendly seasonal context.
- Cartoon only. Handle dengue (মশার রাত) with a hygiene tip, or leave it out.
- Where a game imitates a risky activity, say so in one line: kites near wires, ডাংগুলি near eyes, glass-coated string.

### 6.3 Accessibility baseline

- Controls sit in the bottom 45% of the screen. Targets are at least 44 px, and 56 px for fast taps. Nothing needs two fingers except where a brief says so.
- Every swipe or drag has a tap alternative (WCAG 2.2: pointer gestures and dragging movements [S28]).
- Nothing flashes more than three times a second (WCAG 2.3.1). The antenna noise and the storm are the games to watch.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` turns off shake, particles and parallax and slows defaults [S28]. Add a **visible "ধীরে খেলো" toggle** as well, because many people on cheap phones never touch the OS setting.
- Pause when the tab is hidden (WCAG 2.2.2). Sound is off by default (buses, classrooms, prayer times) and every sound cue also has a visual cue. Colour is never the only signal. Retry takes under a second.

### 6.4 IP checklist for each release

- The name is not a trademark and does not echo one.
- No real brand, logo, uniform, channel, phone or post-office identity, and no music except our own.
- Art is drawn fresh. Mechanics are free but a genre leader's *look and behaviour* are not [S19]: change the geometry, the pieces, the colours and the rule numbers.
- Record, for each game, a one-line "what we avoided". This is not legal advice; for পাটি বুনি and match-3, ask a lawyer or drop the game.

### 6.5 Phones and offline

- Cheap Android is the target: the sub-BDT-20,000 band was about 80% of smartphones in Q1 2024 (the source is promotional content, so treat the number loosely [S25]).
- Canvas 2D, at most about 150 KB gzipped per game route, 30 fps on a 2 GB phone, device pixel ratio capped at 2, no per-frame allocation, no network during play (only the share step), offline once loaded. Test on one low-end Android. Physics games (ক্যারম) cap the object count.

### 6.6 Placement in the site

- Make **গেম** the category for screen games and keep খেলাধুলা for sport and the rules of outdoor games.
- `research/build-queue.md` already types eight live or queued tools as games: শেষ বলে ছয়, বাংলা টাইপিং স্পিড, নামতা চ্যালেঞ্জ, দেশি মিশ্রণ, মামা, যাবেন?, ফ্লেমস, গানের কলি and এক কোটি টাকা খরচ করো. Re-homing them would give the category eight tools on day one. That is the owner's call; I changed nothing.
- Suggested hub look: a shelf of LCD "cartridges" (ideas R13 and R28 as a skin, not as games).
