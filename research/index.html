Our product interpretation

Borrow the clear action.
Find a different need.

Unseen Bangladesh gives users a result about themselves. The output is easy to understand and easy to post. We can test that pattern with a new purpose. [S01] [S02]

  1. 1
    Recognize something personalA memory, a list or a useful local action.
  2. 2
    Make a result in one short visitLet people see the value before asking for details.
  3. 3
    Give them a useful exportA card, checklist or specific invitation.
  4. 4
    Let another person respondSharing stays optional. No forced tags or contacts.

This is a design hypothesis. It is not a formula for virality.

Do not build directly from today’s loudest topic. A match score or celebrity moment can disappear quickly. For the first projects, choose a stable task with a clear social moment. Validate that task with the people who will use it.

How the comparison works

Each idea has five scores. Sharing means a useful reason to pass the result to another person. Use means potential for a repeated task.

Benefit means plausible social value. Ease includes the content and maintenance burden. Evidence means how directly the inspected sources support the problem. A 5 means stronger within this shortlist. A 1 means weaker.

4.00/ 5

বাজার নোট

A household basket, made clear

A household or student needs a clear list and a total before the next bazaar trip.

Share3/5Use5/5Benefit4/5Ease4/5Evidence4/5

3–5 focused build days
Planning estimate. Content and field work can take longer.

Read the full idea brief

Smallest useful version

Enter item, amount, unit and price. See the basket total and budget difference. Export a list. Save locally with a clear delete control.

Why a person could share it

Send the actual shopping list to a household member. Keep budget and totals optional on the export.

What makes it different

Turn the user’s own prices into a useful basket. BazarBoard already targets community price discovery.

What the evidence supports

Recent cost-pressure reporting and quarterly inflation data support the problem. They do not prove people will enter prices manually. [S09] [S10] [S15] [S27] [S28]

Existing alternatives

BazarBoard, Kombeshi, official DAM reports, phone calculators, paper lists and household chats. The proposed task uses the household’s own prices. Current habits remain the main competitor.

A small, real test

Ask 15 households or students to use it for two real shopping trips. At least 8 must choose to use it again.

Limits and care

Do not label entered prices as live market data. Support piece, hali, kg and litre explicitly. Do not promise savings.

Only after the first version works

Add basket comparison only after users keep records. Use dated, attributable external prices only with a reliable data owner.

My application checklist

An applicant needs to keep official links, documents and deadlines in one place.

Share2/5Use5/5Benefit4/5Ease4/5Evidence4/5

3–5 focused build days
Planning estimate. Content and field work can take longer.

Read the full idea brief

Smallest useful version

A private checklist with user-entered deadlines, official links and calendar export. Keep the user’s application details on their device.

Why a person could share it

Export a blank checklist for a study group. Never include application IDs, fees paid or personal files by default.

What makes it different

Personal task completion, not another job-circular feed or eligibility predictor.

What the evidence supports

September reporting describes intense demand for government posts. It does not measure missed deadlines or demand for a tracker. [S11]

Existing alternatives

Calendars, notes, spreadsheets and official application portals. Interviews must show a gap in those tools.

A small, real test

Recruit 15 applicants with real open applications. Observe whether they use the checklist during one deadline cycle.

Limits and care

Wrong dates cause harm. Require a source link and a date check. Calendar export is not a guaranteed reminder service.

Only after the first version works

Add a curated notice feed only with a named editor and a correction process.

আমার ছোটবেলা

My childhood postcard

Help a person express a familiar memory in a form that friends can answer.

Share5/5Use2/5Benefit2/5Ease5/5Evidence3/5

2–3 focused build days
Planning estimate. Content and field work can take longer.

Read the full idea brief

Smallest useful version

Choose games, everyday moments and objects. Add an optional name. Download one Bangla postcard. Keep all inputs in the browser.

Why a person could share it

The card asks: “তোমার ছোটবেলা কেমন ছিল?” A friend can make a different card from the same link.

What makes it different

A personal memory collage. No travel score, identity test or “real Bangladeshi” rank.

What the evidence supports

Nostalgia reports in August 2025 and September 2026, plus October map-sharing reports, support a timely experiment. They do not prove demand. [S03] [S30] [S02]

Existing alternatives

Unseen Bangladesh and generic nostalgia-card tools are adjacent. No dedicated local equivalent surfaced in this bounded search. That does not prove novelty.

A small, real test

Let 20 adults try it without instructions. Then observe at least 100 qualified starts. Look for completion and voluntary sharing.

Limits and care

A one-use novelty can fade fast. Represent different places and generations. Use original art. Names and memories stay private until the user exports them.

Only after the first version works

If people want a useful second action, add a small traditional-play invitation. Do not add a social feed.

এক ঘণ্টা ভালো কাজ

One useful local action

A person wants to help but cannot easily judge which local action fits their time.

Share3/5Use4/5Benefit5/5Ease2/5Evidence3/5

4–6 build days + weekly editor
Planning estimate. Content and field work can take longer.

Read the full idea brief

Smallest useful version

List five organizer-confirmed actions in one city. Show date, duration, conditions and official signup. Expire each listing automatically.

Why a person could share it

Create a simple invitation for friends. Report attendance only when the organizer confirms it.

What makes it different

Help people find a first action across existing organizers. Let established groups manage volunteers.

What the evidence supports

Existing volunteer networks and a newly announced canal initiative show real activity. Demand for a separate discovery site is untested. [S13] [S14]

Existing alternatives

Organizer pages and existing volunteer networks. Partnership is more useful than another volunteer database.

A small, real test

Interview three organizers. Pilot with two willing partners. Confirm at least 10 attendances before expanding.

Limits and care

A short event can need more than one hour. Show the real duration. Do not collect donations, publish volunteer contacts or certify unverified impact.

Only after the first version works

Expand only when an editor and organizer can keep dates, eligibility and cancellation notices current.

    Interaction rules

    1. Let the task start at once. Show the form and a sample result. Keep sign-in out of the first three concepts unless a real need emerges.
    2. Write the next action clearly. Prefer “তালিকা ডাউনলোড করুন” to vague labels. Use familiar units and explain totals.
    3. Make touch and keyboard work. Use visible focus, clear labels, large controls and readable contrast. Never use color as the only signal.
    4. Keep the page light. Avoid autoplay, large video backgrounds and unnecessary client code. Respect reduced-motion preferences.
    5. Explain privacy beside the input. Say what stays on the device and what the export includes. A shared image cannot be recalled.
    6. Check with people. Test Bangla wording, older Android devices, mobile Safari, zoom and slow connections. Treat design preference as a question to investigate.

    Turn criticism into a research question. If someone cannot understand or trust a page, record the task and the visible failure. Do not label a group as impatient, emotional or easy to influence. Find a change you can check.

    05

    Validation / first 14 days

    A small launch.
    A clear reason to continue.

    Finish one complete action before expanding. The targets below are proposed decision rules for this project. They are not industry benchmarks. Set the rules before inviting users.

    DAYS 01–03

    Watch the first use.

    Recruit 20 adults across several situations. Show the postcard without a tutorial. Ask them to think aloud. Notice unclear words, missing memories and export failures.

    Record task outcomes and brief anonymous notes. Ask permission before recording a session. Change only what blocks the main action.

    DAYS 04–07

    Release one small version.

    Finish the prompt set, mobile layout and image export. Check keyboard use, long Bangla text and low-width screens. Publish one clear introduction page.

    Invite people through your own channels and willing community moderators. Use transparent attribution. Do not buy reactions or require tags to unlock the result.

    DAYS 08–14

    Measure a real response.

    Observe at least 100 qualified starts. A qualified start is one consenting target adult who opens the task through the pilot start link. Exclude staff, bots and repeated sessions.

    Use Next.js.

    Next.js is a reasonable full-stack framework for these websites. It can render public content and support focused browser interactions. Start with the parts each product needs. A framework alone does not guarantee search traffic. [S20] [S21]

    One app, twenty focused tools.

    The implemented site now uses one Next.js app. Each tool has its own route and module. Navigation, Bengali fonts, history, image export and SEO use shared components. Read the seven-tool expansion report.

    Chhotobela: a static prompt catalogue, a focused client form and a canvas export. No account or database is needed for the first release.

    Bazar Note: validate amounts, units and prices in the browser. Use integer minor currency units for arithmetic. Save on the device only when the user chooses. Provide export and deletion.

    Ek Ghonta: start with a small, reviewed content file. Give each event an official source, checked date, expiry and contact path. Add a content editor only when the maintenance work requires one.

    Keep data boundaries visible.

    Names and memories
    Do not send them to a server, analytics event, URL or public social preview. The user controls the exported image.
    Household budgets
    Keep them on the device. Explain that local data can disappear if browser storage is cleared. Export is the portable copy.
    Volunteer information
    Link to the organizer’s official signup. Do not build a public contact directory or accept donations in this first tool.
    Optional measurement after launch
    Use anonymous aggregate task events where possible. Do not log entered text. Define retention and deletion before collection.
    Trust at the boundary
    Validate imported data and external links. Escape user text. Check dates and expired content. Keep secrets out of browser bundles.

    Memory brief · Basket brief · Local-action brief

    Grow from a working product.

    First, one page produces a correct result. Then improve the language and mobile experience. Then add a measured second action.

    Do not begin with a common platform, profiles, a feed, payments, a recommendation engine or an AI content pipeline. The current tests do not require those features.

    Use Next.js App Router for each tool route. Keep public pages as Server Components. Put form state and export in a small Client Component. [S20]

    Start browser-only products with static export when it meets their requirements. Add server storage only for a feature that needs it. Use server rendering for time-sensitive event pages. [S36]

    Search and sharing need real content.

    Create unique titles and descriptions. Add a canonical URL, sitemap and clear headings. Write useful Bangla instructions and examples. Use Next.js metadata and Open Graph conventions for a generic preview. [S21]

    Keep personal result pages out of the index. Do not create hundreds of thin district pages to target keywords. If there are two languages, give each a complete page and correct language links.

    Check the full action before launch.

    Test form validation, long names, Bangla output, totals, reset and export failures. Check the main path on actual target phones. For listings, test expired and cancelled events.

    Use automated checks for important logic and a few complete browser flows. Keep a small release checklist. Deploy the simplest version whose main action works from start to finish.

    Free for people still has operating costs. The browser-only tools need hosting and maintenance. The civic tool also needs an editor and partner time. Agree on a small cost limit before public launch. Do not fund the first test by adding intrusive ads or selling personal data.

    07

    Source ledger / open to inspection

    Know what we checked.
    Know what is still uncertain.

    This is a bounded research pass, captured on 9 October 2026. It combines current public pages, older context, product checks and one twelve-month search comparison.

    Evidence and inference are separate.

    Reporting can identify a concrete event or complaint. Product pages show existing supply and advertised features. Official statistics add context. None of these sources proves that a proposed website will succeed. Product adoption claims were not audited.

    Facebook coverage is limited.

    Search-indexed Facebook results were sparse and often stale. We did not access private groups, collect personal profiles or build a representative post dataset. Press reports about Facebook activity remain press reports. Meta’s published explanation says automated collection requires permission. That explanation does not provide access for this project. [S25]

    The search data are a sample of questions.

    The weekly chart uses five specified English search terms and one geography. It does not cover all spellings, Bangla terms or topics. The final week is incomplete. The daily surge table is a separate view, with a limited row sample and bucketed traffic. Avoid percentages of “national interest” from either table.

    Restricted data tools are separate from this report.

    Meta Content Library has controlled research access. Eligible researchers can apply. This project has no access and collected no Facebook post or comment corpus. [S31] [S37]

    Google provides a Trends API alpha with limited access. This project uses public pages. API values use a different scale from the Explore index. [S32]

    The next evidence needs the same scope.

    Use seven-day snapshots for new attention, 90-day views for persistence and twelve-month views for seasonality. Seven-day and 90-day Explore exports remain unchecked in this report.

    Compare Bangla, English and Banglish variants. Use Topics where they fit. Keep each window separate. Do not join 0–100 values from separate windows into one scale. [S38] [S07]

    For permitted public community samples, use a fixed source list. Record language, region, publication date, capture date and task. Deduplicate reposts. Keep criticism and contrary evidence. Engagement alone does not mean approval.

    Some opportunities need more verification.

    DSCC service figures are institutional claims. A planned cleanup is not completed impact. Train-ticket reporting describes specific friction. Fare schedules need primary route verification. These sources inform interviews and scope. They are not ready-made data feeds. [S12] [S13] [S22] [S23]

    Read dates carefully.

    The ledger separates publication dates, data periods and check dates. A current website footer or page masthead does not make an older article current. A linked product can change after this snapshot.
