# একটুখানি: tool ideas from global communities and viral web toys

Research date: 10 Oct 2026. 90 ideas. None repeats the 12 live tools, and none is a district-visited map.

## Owner guidance applied

- **Simplicity score on every row** (last column): 5 = one obvious tap or one input on a cheap phone, 3 = a short flow, 2 or below = feature-heavy. **Lead with 4–5.**
- **Faith:** Ramadan, Eid and Islamic-calendar ideas are included (sections D, E and I). None gives a religious ruling. Anything that needs authoritative data is flagged **[needs authoritative data]**. That covers prayer, sehri and iftar times (Islamic Foundation publishes them), the Hijri date (decided by the national moon-sighting committee), and zakat rules (left out entirely, since a competitor already has one). Hindu, Buddhist and Christian Bangladeshis get their own festival ideas (9, 65, 88, 89), and Eid ideas never score worship.
- **Cricket and FIFA football** are covered in rows 3, 17, 61 and 75–76, plus the new section H (79–84), which leans into Argentina vs Brazil.
- **Niche or heavy, so deprioritise:** 31, 35, 39–42, 45, 47, 48, 63, 64, 77, 78. They solve real but narrow tasks, and several duplicate the competitor.

## Method and limits (read first)

- **Reddit could not be read.** reddit.com refuses this crawler: search returns "domains not accessible to our user agent", and direct fetches fail. So **this file quotes no Reddit threads.** Community signal comes from other places: search-indexed bn.quora.com and quora.com pages (Quora's own search returned 403), Bangladeshi newspapers reporting what people do, App Store listings, Know Your Meme, and search-volume lists.
- Google's Year in Search 2025 has no Bangladesh page ([Google][yis-2025]). Ittefaq's summary of Bangladeshi searches lists "লাইভ স্কোর", AI tools and travel, but no tools or calculators ([Ittefaq][ittefaq-yis]). A third-party keyword list shows huge Bangla volumes for "eng to bng" (11.1M a month), "weather tomorrow" (7.48M) and land.gov.bd (2.24M). Betting sites also rank at the top ([clicks.so][clicks]).
- **A competitor exists.** Teamz Lab has 47 Bangladesh tools ([list][teamz]): bKash fees, GPA, age with job limits, Bangla date, land, gold, zakat, typing test, flag frames and a cattle-market bingo. Each makes a 1080×1080 card ([haat quiz][teamz-haat]). Rows marked **[competitor]** need a card-first, emotional angle, not just a utility.
- Context today: unseenbangladesh went viral in early October 2026, with a self-reported 24M visits and 1.98M unique visitors in one week ([Jagonews24][unseen-jago], [Daily Star][unseen-ds]). Bangladesh has about 72M Facebook users, and people aged 18–24 are the largest group at 28.1M ([NapoleonCat][napoleoncat]).
- Calendar from today: Mahalaya is today. Durga Puja runs 17–21 Oct ([OfficeHolidays][puja-oh]). HSC results are expected 7–15 Nov ([Dhaka Tribune][hsc-dt]). Wedding season runs Nov–Feb ([Daily Star][wed-season]). Victory Day is 16 Dec, and Shakrain is 14 Jan ([Daily Star][sakrain-ds]). The 2027 dates for Ramadan, Eid and Ekushe's 75th anniversary are approximate.
- "Also in trends doc" means the idea also appears in `research/trends-2026-10.md`.

Columns: **name | concept | interaction | shareable output | evidence | Bangla/Banglish search terms | season | risks | simple (5 = one obvious tap)**

## Formats that spread and why

- **One number out of a known total ("X/Y").** It invites comparison and tagging. unseenbangladesh grew when friends asked for their own maps and tagged each other ([Daily Star][unseen-ds], [Jagonews24][unseen-jago]). The Rice Purity score works the same way: people react to scores, compare them, and update them live ([KYM][rice-kym], [Yahoo][rice-yahoo]). *Adaptation:* rows 1–10.
- **Results that are hard to read without context.** Wordle's grid showed the pattern without the answer, so strangers got curious. It came from players and was then automated, and 1.2M grids were shared in a week ([Buildd][wordle-buildd], [Newstalk ZB][wordle-nz]). *Adaptation:* rows 69 and 72.
- **Your data rendered as a familiar object** (receipt, festival poster, pie chart). Receiptify borrowed @albumreceipts' look ([The Star][receiptify]). Instafest arrived just before Wrapped and passed 5M posters ([TechCrunch][instafest-tc]). Spotify Pie spread because it refreshed monthly instead of yearly ([MusicTech][pie]). *Adaptation:* receipts, cassettes, posters and team sheets (52–54, 76). Without Spotify data, the input is a quick pick list.
- **Year-end recaps work only when they're personal.** Wrapped was shared 500M times on day one ([Idomoo][wrapped-idomoo]). Copies fail when they're "creepy, uninteresting, impersonal, or not shareable" ([Substack][wrapped-substack], [UX Planet][wrapped-uxp]). *Adaptation:* row 63, kept short.
- **Fill-in templates (bingo, then punch cards).** They make goals feel like a game, and every card is personal. Pinterest searches for bingo cards beat vision boards in 2025 ([Marie Claire][bingo-mc]). In 2026 the trend moved to punch cards ([Daily Dot][punch-dd], [Fast Company][punch-fc]). *Adaptation:* rows 11–18, which fit Eid, weddings, load-shedding and rain.
- **Curated taste as identity** (starter packs, four favourites, tier lists, 3×3 grids). They're quick to make, they say something about you, and they start arguments ([The Spinoff][four-spinoff], [AOL][starter-aol], [Outer Haven][tier-oh]). The 2025 starter packs were AI-made ([Kapwing][starter-kap]). A hand-drawn, AI-free version avoids the "everyone looks the same" fatigue ([bdnews24][ai-fatigue], via trends doc). *Adaptation:* rows 26 and 55–57.
- **"Which X are you?" quizzes** satisfy curiosity about yourself and give a cheap way to share something personal and belong ([RWU thesis][quiz-rwu], [Gulf News][quiz-gulf]). *Adaptation:* rows 19–25, built on objects that are only ours (tea, bhorta, adda roles).
- **Reframing a number you already know** (life in weeks, age on other planets) ([Cool Infographics][weeks-ci], [Tech & Learning][planets-tl]). *Adaptation:* three calendars and the six seasons (27–28). This is more meaningful in Bangladesh than planets, because Bangladeshis already live with three calendars ([risingbd masthead][cow-rising]).
- **Curious toys that escalate** (neal.fun). Stimulation Clicker was called the "first viral link of 2025" ([Wikipedia][stim-wiki]). Internet Roadtrip spread through collective play ([Wikipedia][roadtrip-wiki]). Infinite Craft was the third most searched game of 2024 ([Schlaff][neal-schlaff]). *Adaptation:* rows 71–73.
- **Avatars and frames.** Picrew spread through artist-made styles and matching profile pictures ([KYM][picrew-kym]). Bangladesh already has a profile-frame culture for national moments ([Jagonews24][red-jago], [nasirkhn][frames-nasir]). *Adaptation:* rows 50 and 60, with photos kept on the device.
- **Emotional anonymous archives** like the Unsent Project ([Grazia][unsent-grazia]) are powerful but need moderation. Row 66 keeps the emotion and drops the public wall.
- **Bangladesh-specific guardrails.** Betting brands dominate Bangladeshi search ([clicks.so][clicks]), so give nothing prizes, odds or stakes. The competitor already covers plain utilities with 1080×1080 cards ([Teamz][teamz]), so একটুখানি wins on 4:5 and 9:16 cards with feeling, crowd comparison and Bangla craft.
