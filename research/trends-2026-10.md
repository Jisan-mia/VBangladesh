# Ektukhani (একটুখানি): Bangladesh trends and share-culture research, Oct 2026

Prepared 10 October 2026. Every factual claim has a source URL next to it. Labels used in this report:

- **[observed]**: I checked it myself on 10 Oct 2026 (a live page, Google Trends, Google autocomplete, or a file I measured).
- **[unverified]**: the source is weak, came from a search snippet I could not open, or the date is projected.
- **[editorial]**: my recommendation, not a sourced fact.

## Executive summary: 10 points Ektukhani can act on

1. **unseenbangladesh is real and very recent.** It went viral on Facebook in the first week of October 2026. The format is simple: tick districts, get "X/64", add a name and photo, download a PNG, JPG or PDF, post it. The two things that made people share were self-disclosure plus comparison ("I've done 30, you?") and national pride. On 10 Oct 2026 its live counter showed **১৪,১৭,১৭৩** people had used the map [observed](https://unseenbangladesh.com). Ektukhani should borrow that grammar (a personal score out of a known total, plus name and photo) rather than its topic. ([Jugantor](https://www.jugantor.com/lifestyle/1164993), [ekhon.tv](https://ekhon.tv/travel-and-tourism/6ac34026262a5b1f4b0896e6))
2. **The audience is young.** 77% of Bangladesh's ~72M Facebook users are 18–34 ([NapoleonCat, Sep 2026](https://napoleoncat.com/stats/facebook-users-in-bangladesh/2026/08/)). Their childhoods fall roughly between 1995 and 2015. Nostalgia content therefore needs to cover the 2000s (Meena, Sisimpur, Hindi-dubbed Doraemon before the 2013 ban, cyber cafés, Nokia phones) as well as 90s BTV.
3. **Design for a 360×800 Android screen inside the Facebook in-app browser.** Android has 93.5% of mobile OS share in Bangladesh, and 360×800 is the most common screen size ([StatCounter OS](https://gs.statcounter.com/os-market-share/mobile/bangladesh), [StatCounter resolution](https://gs.statcounter.com/screen-resolution-stats/mobile/bangladesh)). Image downloads often fail inside the Facebook/Messenger in-app browser ([Twibbonize help](https://help.twibbonize.com/en/articles/14004659-why-can-t-i-download-images-from-an-in-app-browser), [WordPress plugin thread](https://wordpress.org/support/?p=18918363)). A fallback for saving the image is the most valuable single engineering fix for sharing.
4. **Make the image the thing people share, not the link.** Facebook has been testing a cap of 2 organic link posts per month for Pages and professional-mode profiles without Meta Verified ([TechCrunch, Dec 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/12/17/facebook-is-testing-a-link-posting-limit-for-professional-accounts-and-pages)). Users can't add Facebook profile frames any more; only approved organisations can create them, since March 2022 ([Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/meta-announces-new-restrictions-on-the-creation-and-use-of-profile-image-fr/619140/)). Put a small, tasteful `ektukhani` watermark on every output so a reposted image still brings people back.
5. **The next six months are packed with dates to build around.** Durga Puja: Mahashashthi is 16 Oct and Bijoya Dashami is 21 Oct 2026. Nabanna (১ অগ্রহায়ণ) is 16 Nov. Bijoy Dibos is 16 Dec, the **55th** anniversary. Shakrain and Poush Sankranti fall around 14 Jan. **21 Feb 2027 is the 75th anniversary of the 1952 Language Movement, and it falls inside Ramadan.** Ramadan is expected to start around 8–9 Feb, Eid-ul-Fitr around 10 Mar, and Pohela Boishakh **১৪৩৪** on 14 Apr. The BPL is **not** happening this season. (Sources in §3.)
6. **Ramadan 2027 swallows Falgun, Valentine's Day and Ekushe.** Expect the mood to be restrained. The useful tools in that window are a "রোজার বাজারের লিস্ট" (Ramadan grocery list, a real autocomplete query), sehri/iftar planners, Eid salami and Eid cards, and dignified Ekushe posters rather than party themes. [editorial]
7. **People search using words like "ছবি", "ডিজাইন", "লিস্ট", "তালিকা", "২০২৬", "png", "ক্যাপশন".** Examples: "মেট্রোরেলের ভাড়ার তালিকা ২০২৬", "আলপনা ডিজাইন png", "নকশি কাঁথা ডিজাইন ছবি ২০২৬", "শুভ বিজয়া দশমীর শুভেচ্ছা ছবি", "পিঠার নাম ও ছবি", "রোজার বাজারের লিস্ট". Banglish variants are common ("metro rail vara chart") [observed via Google autocomplete, hl=bn gl=bd].
8. **Copy should follow how bKash and Grameenphone actually write.** They use আপনি, short verbs and English loanwords spelled in Bangla: ডাউনলোড করুন, বিস্তারিত জানুন, সব দেখুন, শেয়ার, অফার ([bKash](https://www.bkash.com/bn), [GP](https://www.grameenphone.com/bn)). unseenbangladesh writes the same way: "জেলা বাছাই শুরু করুন", "সব মুছুন", "থিম বেছে নিন" [observed]. Avoid Sanskritised UI words like সম্পাদনা, প্রক্রিয়াকরণ and অবতরণিকা.
9. **Fonts: keep Noto Sans Bengali for body text and add Baloo Da 2 for headlines.** All 11 Bengali families on Google Fonts are OFL [observed]. unseenbangladesh and 10 Minute School both use **Anek Bangla** [observed], so using it would make Ektukhani look like a copy. Baloo Da 2 is rounded and warm, and its Bengali subset is about 41 KB per weight [observed].
10. **Stay away from party politics and religious argument.** Political memes have been a major Facebook genre since July 2024 ([BRAC thesis](https://dspace.bracu.ac.bd/items/20834edf-c278-4fbc-9f6a-38c601d5a147)). Autocomplete for "পূজার শুভেচ্ছা" also suggests "…জানানো কি জায়েজ" ("…is it permissible to give?") [observed]. Keep festival tools opt-in and celebratory, with no edgy humour about religion or politics. [editorial]

## 1. unseenbangladesh: what it is and why it spread

### What it is

- **URL and title:** unseenbangladesh.com, titled "Unseen Bangladesh — বাংলাদেশ ভ্রমণ ম্যাপ, ৬৪ জেলার ভ্রমণ গাইড ও ট্রিপ প্ল্যানার". The OG title is "বাংলাদেশের কতটুকু ঘুরে দেখেছেন?" (How much of Bangladesh have you seen?), og:locale is bn_BD, and the theme colour is #0f6b4f [observed](https://unseenbangladesh.com).
- **Creator:** Md. Sabbir Hossain Robin, an economics graduate of the University of Chittagong. He works in IT and design and has visited about 45 districts himself ([Daily Star via ANN, 7 Oct 2026](https://asianews.network/this-bangladesh-map-has-been-going-viral-but-theres-more-to-it/), [Rupali Bangladesh, 7 Oct 2026](http://www.rupalibangladesh.com/educational-news/news/171297)). The footer credits "Made by Sabbir Hossien" [observed].
- **Build:** Rupali Bangladesh reports he built the first version in about 90 minutes with AI help ([Rupali Bangladesh](http://www.rupalibangladesh.com/educational-news/news/171297)).
- **Features:** the district map; a guide to 240+ attractions; user-submitted "লুকানো রত্ন" (hidden gems) that earn contributor points; a world map of 194/195 countries; quizzes and a district-map puzzle; and a trip planner that orders stops and estimates cost at three comfort levels ([Jugantor, 7 Oct](https://www.jugantor.com/lifestyle/1165535), [ANN](https://asianews.network/this-bangladesh-map-has-been-going-viral-but-theres-more-to-it/)).
- **Stance:** free, with "no intrusive advertising or visitor tracking" ([ANN](https://asianews.network/this-bangladesh-map-has-been-going-viral-but-theres-more-to-it/)).
- **Stack details I observed:** an English toggle, a PWA manifest, and a self-hosted, preloaded `anek-bangla-bengali.woff2`. The meta description says "The original Unseen Bangladesh travel map", which suggests copycats have already appeared [observed].

### When it went viral

- Coverage clusters on **5–7 October 2026**: [ekhon.tv, 5 Oct](https://ekhon.tv/travel-and-tourism/6ac34026262a5b1f4b0896e6), [teachers.gov.bd, 5 Oct](https://www.teachers.gov.bd/blog/details/850895), [Jugantor, 6 Oct](https://www.jugantor.com/lifestyle/1164993), [Daily Star/ANN, 7 Oct](https://asianews.network/this-bangladesh-map-has-been-going-viral-but-theres-more-to-it/).
- **Traffic (all self-reported by Robin):**
  - more than 80,000 visitors in the first week ([ANN](https://asianews.network/this-bangladesh-map-has-been-going-viral-but-theres-more-to-it/));
  - a peak of about 40,000 views every 15 minutes, and more than 2.2M page views and 1.3M visits in a single day (same source);
  - Jagonews24 reportedly gave 24M visits and 1.98M unique visitors in a seven-day window ([Jagonews24 EN](https://www.jagonews24.com/en/sci-tech/news/96937)) [unverified: the page returned 403 to me, so this comes from a search snippet];
  - the live counter on 10 Oct read "১৪,১৭,১৭৩ জন ইতিমধ্যে এই ম্যাপ ব্যবহার করেছেন" (1,417,173 people have already used this map) [observed].

### Core interaction

According to [ekhon.tv](https://ekhon.tv/travel-and-tourism/6ac34026262a5b1f4b0896e6) and [teachers.gov.bd](https://www.teachers.gov.bd/blog/details/850895), and as I observed on the site:

1. Pick districts from a list grouped by division (each group shows something like "ঢাকা বিভাগ ০/১৩" with a "সব বাছাই" button), by searching, or by tapping the map.
2. A live score updates as you go, for example "১৮/৬৪".
3. Choose one of five colour themes.
4. Add a name and a photo. **If no name is entered, the default is "আমার বাংলাদেশ" (My Bangladesh).**
5. Download as PNG, JPG or PDF.

On 10 Oct I saw no share button, only downloads [observed].

### What the shared image looks like

- A map of Bangladesh with the visited districts coloured in, the user's name and photo, and the score. Some maps are almost fully coloured; others show that years of travel cover only a small part of the country ([ANN](https://asianews.network/this-bangladesh-map-has-been-going-viral-but-theres-more-to-it/)).
- In Jugantor's words, someone shows 10 districts, someone 30, someone claims all 64 ([Jugantor](https://www.jugantor.com/lifestyle/1164993)).

### How the share loop worked

1. The download is posted to people's own Facebook timelines.
2. Others compare it with their own travels.
3. They make their own map and often challenge friends to do the same ([ekhon.tv](https://ekhon.tv/travel-and-tourism/6ac34026262a5b1f4b0896e6)).

The seed was Robin sharing his own map with friends, who wanted one. It then spread through travel groups and personal posts ([ANN](https://asianews.network/this-bangladesh-map-has-been-going-viral-but-theres-more-to-it/), [Jugantor](https://www.jugantor.com/lifestyle/1164993)). A single Facebook post took thousands of people to the site in one night, and he had to upgrade hosting ([teachers.gov.bd](https://www.teachers.gov.bd/blog/details/850895)).

### Why people shared

- **Identity and participation.** Jugantor's analysis is that it gives people a chance to show their personal identity and take part. Comparing who has more districts creates curiosity, and the blank districts prompt plans for future trips ([Jugantor](https://www.jugantor.com/lifestyle/1164993)).
- **Pride in the country.** It tapped a wish to explore one's own country and filled a gap: there was no such tool in Bangla. Robin: "It started as a hobby. I could not find anything like it in Bangla, so I made one myself" ([ANN](https://asianews.network/this-bangladesh-map-has-been-going-viral-but-theres-more-to-it/)).
- **Discovery.** Robin: "What moves me most is seeing people discover districts they had never thought of visiting" (same source).
- **Low friction.** It was free, had no ads, and kept photos on the device ([viralxt listing](https://viralxt.com/unseen-bangladesh-travel-map), [unverified]).
- **What I couldn't find:** individual user posts or comments quoted in news coverage, and any memes or criticism of the trend. Facebook posts need a login, which I did not use.

### Lessons for Ektukhani [editorial]

- **Use a fixed total.** "X/64" is a simple, comparable number. Any Ektukhani tool with a known total can produce the same kind of brag-worthy number: childhood items ticked off, alpana motifs used, pitha tried.
- **Make the output personal and default it to national identity.** Name and photo come first, and the default caption is "আমার বাংলাদেশ". Ektukhani's equivalent default could be "আমার ছোটবেলা" or "আমাদের পাড়া".
- **Let the empty slots do the work.** The uncoloured districts carry the emotional load of what you haven't done yet.
- **Show social proof in Bangla numerals.** For example, "১৪,১৭,১৭৩ জন ইতিমধ্যে…".
- **Avoid travel maps and district checklists.** That ground is now owned by unseenbangladesh and its clones. Build a complementary piece instead: district pride expressed through food, dialect or rickshaw art.

[editorial] Tuning by audience:

- **Friends (তুই/তুমি banter):** let users pick a playful line. Never apply তুই automatically.
- **Parents and relatives on imo or WhatsApp:** respectful, image-only cards.
- **Tag mechanics:** printed prompts like "কাকে ট্যাগ করবেন?" (Who will you tag?) or "তোর ছোটবেলার বন্ধুকে পাঠা" (Send it to your childhood friend) work as caption suggestions, not forced actions.

### Formats

- **Feed:** 4:5 at 1080×1350. **Story/Reels:** 9:16 at 1080×1920. Carousels crop to 1:1 ([First Pier](https://www.firstpier.com/resources/facebook-ad-sizes)).
- **Story safe zones:** keep about 14% at the top and 20–35% at the bottom free of text ([Billo](https://billo.app/blog/meta-ads-safe-zones)) [third-party].
- **OG link card:** 1200×630, as unseenbangladesh uses [observed].
- **Profile frames:** Facebook-native frames are effectively gone for ordinary creators, since only government and health bodies can create them ([Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/meta-announces-new-restrictions-on-the-creation-and-use-of-profile-image-fr/619140/)). A web tool that outputs a framed **square** PNG is the replacement.
- **Text overlays [editorial]:** one large Bangla line of 6–10 words, the name, and a number or score. Keep Bangla text at least about 48px at 1080 width, and make sure conjuncts stay legible after Facebook recompresses the image.

### Devices, browsers and cost

- **OS:** Android 93.47%, iOS 6.46% (Sep 2026) ([StatCounter](https://gs.statcounter.com/os-market-share/mobile/bangladesh)).
- **Browsers:** Chrome 87.8%, Safari 4.4%, Opera 2.8%, UC 2.3%, Samsung 2.0% ([StatCounter](https://gs.statcounter.com/browser-market-share/mobile/bangladesh)). StatCounter doesn't break out the Facebook in-app browser, so its share is unknown [unverified].
- **Screens:** 360×800 (14.2%), 393×873 (9.5%), 385×854, 384×832, 412×915 ([StatCounter](https://gs.statcounter.com/screen-resolution-stats/mobile/bangladesh)).
- **Connectivity:** median mobile download is 36.84 Mbps, but internet penetration is only 47% ([DataReportal](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-bangladesh)).

A 2025 commentary argues data is expensive relative to purchasing power ([Future Startup](https://futurestartup.com/2025/09/11/how-complexity-became-telecos-most-profitable-product/)). I found no current 2026 per-GB figure [unverified].
- **In-app browser:**
  - Facebook and Messenger in-app browsers ignore normal download handling ([WordPress thread](https://wordpress.org/support/?p=18918363)).
  - Blob-URL downloads fail in Android WebViews ([ProAndroidDev](https://proandroiddev.com/blob-downloads-not-working-in-android-web-view-heres-the-real-fix-243144a2a426)).
  - Recommended workarounds: open the image directly, or prompt "Open in Chrome" ([Twibbonize](https://help.twibbonize.com/en/articles/14004659-why-can-t-i-download-images-from-an-in-app-browser)).

## 6. Natural Bangla copywriting

### How loved brands actually write [observed on 10 Oct 2026]

- **bKash** ([bkash.com/bn](https://www.bkash.com/bn)):
  - English service names spelled in Bangla: সেন্ড মানি, ক্যাশ আউট, মোবাইল রিচার্জ, পে বিল, অ্যাড মানি.
  - CTAs: "বিস্তারিত জানুন", "সব দেখুন", "আরো অফার দেখুন", "বিকাশ অ্যাপ ডাউনলোড করুন".
  - Uses আপনি ("আপনার ব্যবসায়িক লেনদেন…").
  - Exclamation used warmly: "ক্যাশ ইন করলে ক্যাশ আউটে খরচ কমবে!" (Cash in and your cash-out costs drop!).
  - Some formal words remain: "তথ্য হালনাগাদ" (update information).
- **Grameenphone** ([grameenphone.com/bn](https://www.grameenphone.com/bn)):
  - "আরও জানুন", "দেখুন", "শপ দেখুন".
  - Personal and short: "আপনার জন্য তৈরি অফারটি খুঁজে নিন" (Find the offer made for you), "আপনার জন্য পারফেক্ট ইন্টারনেট প্যাক" (The perfect internet pack for you).
  - Mixes Latin-script English freely ("1 Bangladesh 1 Internet").
- **10 Minute School** ([10minuteschool.com](https://10minuteschool.com/)):
  - Headline: "শেখা হোক আনন্দে!" (Let learning be joyful!).
  - CTA: "এক্সপ্লোর করুন".
  - Switches to **তুমি** for students: "…একজন হও তুমিও" (…you be one of them too).
  - Mixes in IELTS, Spoken English, MCQ in Latin script.
- **unseenbangladesh** [observed]:
  - Headline: "বাংলাদেশের কতটুকু ঘুরে দেখেছেন?"
  - CTA: "জেলা বাছাই শুরু করুন ↓".
  - Controls: "সব বাছাই করুন / সব মুছুন", "থিম বেছে নিন", "আপনার ছবি যোগ করুন", "আপনার ম্যাপ ডাউনলোড করুন".
  - Tip: "টিপস: ম্যাপের জেলায় সরাসরি ক্লিক করেও বাছাই করতে পারেন।"

**Pattern [editorial]:**

- Use আপনি for general UI.
- Use তুমি in kids' and student contexts, and in playful, opt-in caption lines.
- Write verb-first CTAs in the form "[noun] [verb] করুন".
- Keep English loanwords people already say (ডাউনলোড, শেয়ার, লিংক, থিম, ছবি, অ্যাপ) and use Bangla numerals in content.
- Short sentences, one exclamation at most.

### Bangladesh vs West Bengal usage

- **Sources:** Bangladeshi Bengali leans Perso-Arabic and West Bengal leans Sanskrit, roughly along the Muslim/Hindu majority split ([Wikipedia: Bengali vocabulary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bengali_vocabulary)). The best-documented pair is পানি/জল ([Liberty Wingspan essay](https://libertywingspan.com/52757/ae/bengali-bites-pani-vs-jol/)). গোসল/স্নান appears only in partisan lists [weak source]. দাওয়াত is listed as a synonym of নিমন্ত্রণ ([Wiktionary](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E0%A6%A8%E0%A6%BF%E0%A6%AE%E0%A6%A8%E0%A7%8D%E0%A6%A4%E0%A7%8D%E0%A6%B0%E0%A6%A3)).
- **Guidance [editorial]:**
  - **Default to Bangladeshi forms in the interface:** পানি, গোসল, দাওয়াত, নাশতা, আব্বু/আম্মু next to বাবা/মা, মেহমান next to অতিথি, ভাইয়া/আপু next to দাদা/দিদি, and Bangladesh's revised calendar dates.
  - **Avoid having to pick a side** by writing around the word: "রান্নার উপকরণ" instead of either word for water, or "শুভেচ্ছা" (neutral) rather than religiously marked greetings in generic UI.
  - **Respect each festival's own vocabulary.** A Puja card should say "শুভ বিজয়া", "অঞ্জলি", "প্রতিমা", and nobody should "correct" a Hindu user's "জল" in their own recipe.
  - **Treat religious content as opt-in.** Autocomplete shows people asking whether giving Puja greetings is permissible ("পূজার শুভেচ্ছা জানানো কি জায়েজ") [observed]. The government framing is "ধর্ম যার যার, উৎসব সবার" (each to their own religion, the festival belongs to all) ([Daily Star Bangla](https://bangla.thedailystar.net/abroad/news-526226)). Offer festival cards for everyone, but never push them on anyone.

### Recommended pairing [editorial]

**Body: Noto Sans Bengali (keep it). Headlines and display: Baloo Da 2.**

- **Why Noto for body:** it is already shipped, has the most complete conjunct engineering, comes in 9 weights, and holds up at 14–16px on cheap screens. To save about 20 KB, switch from the variable file (108 KB) to two static weights, 400 and 700, at about 44 KB each. That only pays off if no other weights are used.
- **Why Baloo Da 2 for headlines:** rounded terminals read as warm and handmade, matching the emotional, "একটুখানি" (a little bit) brand. It is distinct from Anek Bangla, which the viral competitor uses. 600–800 weights work for big poster text inside generated images. It is 41 KB per weight, or 90 KB variable.
- **Optional third font, loaded only inside the postcard and greeting tools:** Galada or Tiro Bangla for the "letter or nostalgia" feel. Self-host and lazy-load it so the home page doesn't pay for it.

**Search patterns [editorial from the data]:**

- Intent words: **ছবি / পিক / png / ডিজাইন** (visual), **তালিকা / লিস্ট / চার্ট / pdf** (reference), **ক্যাপশন / স্ট্যাটাস / শুভেচ্ছা বার্তা** (words to post), **২০২৬/2026** (freshness).
- **কত / কবে / কি** questions: "কত", "কবে", "কোন মাসে".
- Banglish is spelled many ways: vara, bhara and bara; alpona and alpana. "ঈদ কার্ড" autocompletes into "id card", so pair Bangla with the English "eid card".
- "কোলাজ" (collage) autocompletes to "কোলাজেন" (collagen). Use "ছবি জোড়া / photo collage" instead.

### Ranking tips

- **FAQ rich results are gone.** Google stopped showing them on 7 May 2026; the notice is dated 8 May on the [Google FAQPage docs](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/faqpage) [observed]. Keep on-page Q&A for users and AI answers, but don't expect SERP snippets.
- **hreflang:** for a Bangla-only site with no alternates, hreflang isn't needed. Google says it uses neither hreflang nor `lang` to detect language ([Google: localized versions](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/international/localized-versions)). Still set `<html lang="bn">` (or `bn-BD`) and `og:locale bn_BD`, as unseenbangladesh does [observed]. Add `hreflang="bn-BD"` plus `x-default` only if an English version ever ships.
- **Title length:** Google truncates by pixels, about 580–600px desktop and about 480px mobile ([Zyppy](https://zyppy.com/title-tags/meta-title-tag-length/)) [third-party]. Bangla glyphs are wide, so keep titles to about 30–40 Bangla characters with the keyword first, for example "মেট্রোরেলের ভাড়ার তালিকা ২০২৬ — হিসাব করুন | একটুখানি" [editorial]. Meta descriptions: about 1–2 short Bangla sentences containing one Banglish variant.
- **Content shape [editorial]:**
  - Put a static fare table above the calculator (the "তালিকা/চার্ট/pdf" intent).
  - Make gallery pages of real outputs: "আলপনা ডিজাইন ছবি" and "নকশি কাঁথা ডিজাইন" with downloadable PNGs.
  - Add festival landing pages with a dated year in the H1 and URL slug, refreshed annually.
  - Offer ready-to-copy caption blocks next to every card ("ক্যাপশন" intent).
  - Add a short "অনুচ্ছেদ"-style explainer, around 120 words, on festival pages. Students search these heavily.
- **Search engine share:** Google had 97.75% of Bangladesh search share in Mar 2024 (Statista via search) [unverified]. Only Google matters.
