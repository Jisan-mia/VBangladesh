**How to read this file**
- ⟨ ⟩ means fill from your own real result or choice. Never post a number, score, speed or GPA the tool did not give you. Never post a child line ("আমার বাচ্চা") unless it is true.
- `{SITE}` is the domain not chosen yet. `?src=` names the channel (fb-foot, fb-cric, fb-bhasha, fb-edu, fb-moms, fb-hsc, fb-family, fb-typing, fb-useful, ig, quora, reddit, li).
- Almost every community in the channel files is a Page (admin-only). Send the admin the guide's আপনি message first and offer the card as a native image. A group that bans links gets the card image only. Post the card natively and put the link in the first comment, from a normal personal profile.
- One tool per community at a time (brief), at least 14 days between posts in the same community and 30 days before the same tool returns (README §7). Many football and cricket pages fit several tools below, so use the overlap table.
- Posting window (channels-other §9): Dhaka 8:30 to 10:30 pm; Fri and Sat late morning; never Fri 12:30 to 2:30 pm; in Ramadan after tarawih, never around iftar.
- Register: fan, campus and youth pages get no pronoun or তোমরা/তোমার. Moms' groups and mixed-age Bangla-language groups get আপনারা/আপনার (replies in them use আপনি). HSC pages talk to students with তুমি or no pronoun.
- **Sports rules, all ten sports tools:** fan energy and friendly banter, never one team or one fan base "better", never a jab at a rival fan or country, and no gambling vocabulary at all (the three words the brief bans; not even "no betting" as a joke). The money line is "টাকা-পয়সার কিছু নেই". Never post after a painful defeat.
- **Hero marks:** only `argentina-na-brazil` is a hero (README §3.1 #9). It alone has the English version.
- **Fixtures, never invented.** Football: check fixtures. calendar.md found no confirmed Argentina or Brazil match in this window (a July report mentions an Argentina break of 9 to 17 Nov 2026 with no opponents named; that is not a fixture). Cricket windows in calendar.md come from Wikipedia and must be rechecked on the BCB or ESPNcricinfo schedule before use: West Indies in Bangladesh (1st Test 28 Oct to 1 Nov Dhaka, 2nd Test 5 to 9 Nov Sylhet), Bangladesh in South Africa (Tests 15 to 19 and 23 to 27 Nov; ODIs 1, 4, 7 Dec; T20Is 10, 12, 13 Dec). Cricket and football pages are wave C (3 to 16 Dec); calendar.md puts the DMs by Fri 20 Nov for the South Africa Tests and by Tue 24 Nov for the white-ball series, README by Thu 26 Nov at the latest. Before wave C, only our own Page. Post the day before a match, never after a defeat.
- **Language and school moments:** Ekushe 21 Feb 2027 is the 75th, solemn: post 17 to 19 Feb after tarawih, nothing on the day, no "challenge" wording. Bijoy Dibos 16 Dec: post 12 to 15 Dec, none on 14 Dec. HSC 2026 results: date not announced, calendar.md says check. DU applications 11 to 25 Nov and tests 5 to 26 Dec: no noisy student campaigns. SSC 2027 written exams 14 Mar to 15 Apr: quiet.
- Tools that finish with a score card each have a "সবার সাথে মেলাও" tally. It is opt-in and shows percentages only past a minimum. Never quote "X% people" unless the tool's own screen shows it.

**রিল আইডিয়া** (about 15 s, hands and screen only, no children's faces)
1. The phone screen, a thumb taps "৬ জন" and "উঠোনে". On screen: "কয়জন, কোথায়?"
2. Tap "হাতে কী আছে? চক". A game card appears with its simple rules.
3. Scroll the rules slowly, one line at a time.
4. Fill the invite (আজ, বিকেল ৪টায়) and show the picture. On screen: "আসছ তো?"
5. End text: "ফোন রেখে উঠোনে নামো। লিংক বায়োতে"

**কমেন্টের উত্তর**
- "এটা কি অনলাইনে খেলা যায়?" → "না, সামনাসামনি খেলার জন্য। এখানে খেলা বাছবেন, নিয়ম পড়বেন, দাওয়াত পাঠাবেন। তারপর ফোন রেখে উঠোনে নামবেন।"
- "আমাদের এলাকায় নিয়মটা অন্যরকম" → "ঠিক বলেছেন, এলাকা ভেদে নিয়ম আর নাম বদলায়। এখানে শুধু একটা সহজ রূপ আছে। শুরুতেই সবাই মিলে নিজেদের নিয়ম ঠিক করে নিলেই হয়।"
- "বাচ্চাদের নাম কি কোথাও থাকে?" → "দাওয়াতের ছবিটা আপনার ফোনেই বানানো হয়, নাম না দিলেও চলে। লিংক বানালে শুধু ওই লিংকের জন্য কথাগুলো থাকে। তাই বাসার পুরো ঠিকানা না লিখে ‘স্কুলের মাঠে’-র মতো চেনা নাম লিখবেন।"
