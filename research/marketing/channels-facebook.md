# Facebook channels map for একটুখানি

Researched 11 Oct 2026. Read-only desk research: I never logged in, joined, posted or messaged anyone. All web content below is treated as data.

## 1. Summary

- Facebook has about 73M Bangladeshi users (NapoleonCat, Feb 2026), but search engines index mostly **Pages** (admin-only posting). Real **Groups** are poorly indexed. The big groups we could read are: FoodBank (about 2.5M), বুকলেট পাঠক ফোরাম (186K), Traffic Alert (451K) and Movie Lovers Polapain (251K). The owner must join groups with his own account and read the pinned rules before any post.
- What gets removed: off-topic links and self-promotion (Traffic Alert rule 5), download or unofficial links (Movie Lovers Polapain), and anything unapproved in admin-approved groups (FoodBank, Cast Away on the Moon). Groups can also vanish: Cast Away on the Moon lost its 500K+ group in July 2021 (Prothom Alo).
- What tends to work: a native image or a short personal memory first, the link second (in a comment, only where allowed), and one tool per community. Ask admins first, openly saying it is a small free project.
- Pages (Euphoria Whisper, নব্বই দশক, Old Dhaka, meme pages) take no member posts. The route is a polite DM or email to the admin. No paid promotion unless the owner decides.
- Diaspora: the Gulf, Malaysia, Singapore, Italy, the UK and the US have large Bangla-named pages (100K to 670K), but nearly all are news or service pages. Job and visa scams are common there, so a "free, no signup, no money" line matters.
- Facebook has been testing a 2 organic link posts per month cap for some Pages (TechCrunch, via `research/trends-2026-10.md`). Plan image-first posts.
- Rows: 216 listed (about 207 distinct, as a few appear in two sections). **197 V, 9 S, 10 U.** Per section: 2.1 has 15, 2.2 has 17, 2.3 has 15, 2.4 has 14, 2.5 has 14, 2.6 has 11, 2.7 has 10, 2.8 has 13, 2.9 has 7, 2.10 has 9, diaspora 54, students 9, parenting 4, other topics 12, big general pages 12. Only 8 are confirmed Groups read live; the rest are Pages.
- Biggest gaps: no sizeable Bangladeshi riddle group, no verified Eid-shopping group, no verified moms' group URL, nothing large for Oman, Canada or Australia. Section 7 lists the exact Facebook search words to find them while logged in.

**How to read the tables.** Confidence: **V** = I saw the URL and name (search result or live page). **S** = only in a search snippet or an article. **U** = likely exists, not confirmed (no URL given). Size: **(f)** = read from the live page on 11 Oct 2026. **(s)** = search snippet or article, date unknown and probably stale. **?** = unknown or not visible. "Page" means only admins post. Many "groups" below are really Pages even when their names say "group" or "club".

## 6. Approach notes per community type

- **Every group:** read the pinned rules and About first. Facebook lets admins require approval for each post and for first posts by new participants (Facebook Help, [manage posts](https://www.facebook.com/help/131887213640898) and [manage membership](https://www.facebook.com/help/214260548594688)). The owner joins with his own real account. Never use the same text in two groups on the same day. One tool per community.
- **Asking admins:** a short DM in plain Bangla (written with `bangla-voice-guide.md`): who we are, that it is free, no signup, no ads, no data collected, and "may I share one picture". Offer to take the post down if they say so. Wait for a yes.
- **90s and nostalgia pages and groups** (`amar-chhotobela`, `cassette`, `tokhon-ekhon`): lead with one real memory, as text or one image, then the tool in a comment if allowed. These communities love "you'll understand if you're a 90s kid" posts (Kathmandu Post). Nostalgia pages are one-person or small teams, so DM them like a fan.
- **Book and movie groups** (`char-priyo`, `himu`): several are shop-run (বুকলেট, 99 - BOOK SHOP), so expect "no ads". Movie Lovers Polapain bans download links outright and discourages links in general. Post the result card as an image, ask a question ("what are your four?"), and keep the link out or in a comment only if allowed.
- **Riddle pages** (`dhadha`): post the riddle as text, put the link only in a comment if the page allows it. Many Bangla riddle pages are West Bengal based, so check the audience is Bangladeshi.
- **Food groups** (`mishtir-baksho`, `jibh`, `mache-bhate`): FoodBank wants reviews and photos with admin approval for promotion and links. Share a real photo story ("which sweet did you eat last Eid?"), not a link.
- **Moms and parents** (`namta`, `boyos`): trust is everything and misinformation worries are real. Pitch `namta` as a free tool for kids' practice. Never touch medical topics.
- **Dhaka commuter groups:** Traffic Alert forbids off-topic and promo posts. Do not post `jam` there. Use meme and general pages instead.
- **Diaspora pages and groups** (`probash`, `deshe-fera`, `chithi`): job and visa scams are widespread there, so be clear that it is free and asks for no money, no passport and no login. Page admins often run help desks; ask before posting. Time posts to Eid and Ramadan.
- **Cricket and football fans** (`dom-atkano`, `argentina-na-brazil`, `amar-jersey`): post on match days or just after, but never after a painful defeat. No mocking rival fans. Keep the Argentina vs Brazil tone friendly.
- **Students** (`gpa`): HSC results around 7 to 15 Nov. Present `gpa` as a calculator, not a flex or a ranking, because results are a stressful time.
- **Puja and Islamic pages** (`mondop`, `sehri-iftar`, `iftar-plate`): ask the page admin first, credit official sources (Islamic Foundation for times), keep wording inclusive and respectful. Never post into a comment thread uninvited.
- **Meme and general pages:** DM an admin with the card as an image, say it is our free project, and let them decide on credit. No paid promotion unless the owner decides.
- **Timing hooks** (from `research/trends-2026-10.md`): Puja 17 to 21 Oct; HSC results 7 to 15 Nov; Nabanna 16 Nov; wedding season Nov to Feb; Victory Day 16 Dec; Shakrain 14 Jan; Ramadan about 8 to 9 Feb 2027; Ekushe 21 Feb 2027 (75th anniversary); Eid-ul-Fitr about 10 Mar 2027.
