# Marketing brief for একটুখানি

## The product
একটুখানি is 86 free, small, Bangla-only interactive tools. Each takes about a minute: no signup, an instant result, and a beautiful card to share on Facebook or Instagram (feed and story sizes). A shared link reopens the exact card with a "এবার তোমার পালা" banner, and that loop is the growth engine. The flagship is আমার ছোটবেলা (a nostalgia checklist). The full list by category is in `research/marketing/tools.md`. Each tool's brief is in `research/build-queue.md` §5, and research evidence is in `research/ideas-*.md` and `research/trends-2026-10.md`.

- **Audience:** ordinary Bangladeshis 16–40 on cheap Android phones, arriving mostly from Facebook. Also the Bangla-speaking diaspora (Gulf, Malaysia, Singapore, UK, US, Canada, Australia, Italy, Japan, Korea).
- **Faith and culture:** about 90% Muslim, with Hindu, Buddhist and Christian minorities. Eid, Ramadan, Puja, Boishakh, Ekushe, Bijoy Dibos. Cricket and FIFA football (Argentina vs Brazil) are huge.
- **Domain:** not chosen yet. Write links as `{SITE}/<slug>`.
- **Analytics:** none today (privacy promise: no tracking cookies). The only server data are anonymous share-link counts and opt-in tallies.

## The owner's goals
- Launch everywhere the right people are, community by community. Each tool has its natural home: a nostalgia tool goes to 90s-kids groups, a book tool to book lovers' groups, riddles to riddle pages, cricket to cricket fan groups, and so on.
- A researched, well-funnelled plan, then execution. Later, an agent (with the owner's accounts and approval) will post.
- Every post, status and caption is in natural, emotional, real Bangla: no robot tone, no exaggeration, no ad voice. Follow `research/bangla-voice-guide.md` and `research/bangla-copy-review.md`.

## Hard rules (ethics and platform rules)
- **No spam.** Read and follow every group's rules. Many BD groups ban links or self-promotion: ask the admin first, or post the image natively with the link in the first comment only where that's allowed.
- **No fake accounts, no sockpuppets, no fake reviews or engagement, no buying likes.** Be honest that it's our own free project.
- **Pace it.** Never post the same text in many groups at once (Facebook flags it, and it's rude). One tool per community at a time, adapted to that community.
- **No politics.** No party, hartal, election or religious-debate groups; never ride a tragedy or a controversy.
- **Respect faith.** For festival tools, post in inclusive ways. Never mock.
- **Privacy:** never ask people for personal data. The tools need none.
