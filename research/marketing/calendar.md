## How to read this

**Date labels**
- **✓** fixed national day or date in a government list or an official schedule I found on 11 Oct 2026 (source key in brackets).
- **সম্ভাব্য** moon-dependent or panjika-based, or a projection. Update when the Islamic Foundation or National Moon Sighting Committee announces, or when the Cabinet Division publishes the 2027 holiday list (due Oct–Nov 2026). The agent's source for these is `tools/koto-din-baki/lib/countdown.ts` and `tools/mondop/lib/mondop.ts`, which the developer keeps in step.
- **check** not announced or not verified. Do not plan a post on it until the owner checks the source.

**Columns.** *Prep by* = copy approved, card tested on the real phone, and admin DMs already sent (send DMs at least 7 days before a post that needs consent). *Post* = the window, not a command. Every post still needs the owner's approval that day and the community's rules checked. Default time: 8:30–10:30 pm Dhaka; weekends late morning; never Friday 12:30–2:30 pm; in Ramadan after tarawih, never around iftar.

**Waves** (A to E) are the Phase 2 community waves in README §4. Moments below add to them; they do not replace them.
