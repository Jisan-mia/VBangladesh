# চুড়ির বাজার: rewrite design

## Look (written before styling, `unslop-ui`)
- **Reference:** a চুড়িপট্টি stall under a hurricane lamp: a velvet-lined tray, glass rings with gold wire, a brass edge, red cloth tags with a price in white. Not a candy shop.
- **Palette, one per মেলা (the velvet):** crimson `#5c0f26`, bottle green `#124a3a`, indigo `#1b2757`, plum `#33163f`; brass `#f3cd6a` on all; ink `#2a1620` on a pale rose paper `#f8edf0` that tints green, blue-grey and mauve with the মেলা. Each bangle colour has its own pattern as well (gold lines, white twist, beads, groove, zigzag, stones), so colour is never the only clue.
- **Shape:** round only what is round (the bangles, the medallion, the padlock's shackle). Buttons and cards are 6 px, tags and chips 4 px, sheets 8 px: a stall's tickets, not pills. Icons are SVG (✓, ✕, the padlock); the only glow is the moves-low edge, amber then red, marked `unslop-ignore`.
- **Type:** the site's own Display and Bangla faces. First action: the medallion that says খেলো.

## Core
- **Concept:** a glass-bangle stall at the মেলা. A 7×7 velvet tray of round glass bangles, six colours, each with its own pattern. Swap two neighbours; three of a colour in a line lift away and new bangles drop in.
- **Fantasy:** you are minding the চুড়িওয়ালা খালা's stall, filling her orders (the goals) from a village fair to the last night of the মেলা.
- **Loop:** *বদলাও* (swap two neighbours, answered the same frame) → *মেলাও* (a line of three lifts, the glass shatters and chimes, new bangles fall, a cascade climbs a note each step) → *বানাও* (four make a ঝিকমিক, five a রংধনু; the goals fill).

## Progression: 4 মেলা × 10 levels (the 40 level numbers and the saved stars stay)
Each level teaches exactly one new thing (its **নতুন** line, shown on the title and as the first status line). The tenth of every মেলা is a named finale with a boss row. Trays are seeded: the seed of each level is the median of nine seeds the greedy bot can clear, so no level rides on a lucky tray. Levels are data in `lib/ladder.ts` (authored) and `lib/ladder-seeds.ts` (generated by `scripts/make-levels.ts`).

| মেলা | Velvet | New things, one per level | Finale (10) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **১ গ্রামের মেলা** (crimson) | 4 then 5 colours | swap → a fifth colour → cascades → two goals → ঝিকমিক (4 in a line) → its blast (two cells each way) → tight moves → a big goal → three goals | **বড় বায়না** |
| **২ বৈশাখী মেলা** (green) | 5 colours | রংধনু (5 in a line; takes a whole colour) → using it → **তালা** (tied bangles don't move, lift in a line; goal "free N") → tied + colour goal → ঝিকমিক frees ties → six ties → tight + ties → রংধনু frees ties → three goals with ties | **নববর্ষের ভিড** |
| **৩ চুড়িপট্টি** (indigo) | 6 colours | a sixth colour (সাদা) → two goals → **ধুলো** (lifting on a dusty cell wipes a layer; goal "wipe N") → dust + colour → **ঘন ধুলো** (two layers) → dust + ties → রংধনু sweeps dust → tight + dust → dust + ties + colour | **বিয়ের বায়না** |
| **৪ রাতের মেলা** (plum) | 6 colours | **তালা পড়ে** (every 7th move a তালা falls on a cell shown one move ahead by a red ring) → free the fallen ones → every 5 moves → falls with dust → falls with ঝিকমিক → ties from the start → tight → রংধনু → all three | **মেলা ভাঙার আগে** |

- **Stars:** ★ for the goals, ★★ and ★★★ by points (the bonus is 50 per move left, counted against the normal move limit). The two thresholds come from fifteen "person" runs of the greedy bot (`lib/hint.ts thresholds`).
- **Unlocks:** your own open level (`ektukhani-churir-bazar-open`, set once from the saved stars by the old rule) opens the next level only when you clear your own open level. A friend's far-ahead level records its stars and opens nothing in between.
- **Bots (`tests/bot.ts`, asserted in `tests/levels.test.ts`):**
  - the **greedy bot** (what a person sees: the first lift only) clears every level inside its moves, with the recorded par and score; it is the solver that proves each level fair;
  - a **casual** bot (one of the best three swaps, one swap in eight any swap) and a **steady** bot (one of the best two, one in twenty any swap) play every level with 40 personality seeds.
  - **Asserted:** level 1 is won in ≤ 2 swaps by the best player and in a median of ≤ 3 by the casual bot (about 5 s); casual ≥ 90% on all of the first মেলা; casual ≥ 40% and steady ≥ 50% on every level; each finale needs more moves than a middling level of its মেলা and is no easier for the casual bot than its first level; the last finale is the longest and harder than the first.
  - **Measured (casual / steady, 30 seeds):** মেলা ১: 100/100% on every level; মেলা ২: 67–100 / 77–100%; মেলা ৩: 63–100 / 57–100%; মেলা ৪: 47–97 / 57–97%.

## Difficulty and story
- **Modes (title chips, remembered on the phone):**
  - **সহজ:** "চাল দেড় গুণ, ইশারা তাড়াতাড়ি আসে। তারা বড়জোর ২টা।"
  - **স্বাভাবিক (default):** "আসল খেলা। ৩ তারা পেতে কম চালে, বেশি পয়েন্টে।"
  - **কঠিন:** "চাল কম, ইশারা নেই। বাকি চালের পয়েন্টও কম, তাই ৩ তারা আরও শক্ত।"
  The tray, its seed and its levels never change with the mode, so a friend's link is the same level; the link names the mode (`d=`) and the move limit is the mode's.
- **Story (first run only, never on a friend's link, tap for the next, hold 0.6 s to skip):**
  1. "বৈশাখের মেলা। খালার দোকানে মখমলের ওপর কাচের চুড়ি।"
  2. "এক রঙের তিনটা পাশাপাশি এলে উঠে যায়। ওপর থেকে নতুন নামে।"
  3. "খালার বায়না তুলে দাও। দোকান থেকে দোকান, মেলা থেকে মেলা।"

## Screens
- **Title:** the biggest thing is a gold-rimmed velvet medallion with the level's first goal as a big bangle in it and **খেলো** on a red ribbon; it bobs gently (after a 2.5 s rest). Under it: ‹ লেভেল ১০: বড় বায়না ›, the নতুন line, the goals and the star points, the mode chips with the chosen one's line, সব লেভেল ★ n/১২০. A friend's card and claim sit on top.
- **Choice moments (all pause play, all numbered):**
  - **খালার উপহার** (before each finale, when you press খেলো): three cards, keys 1–3, each with an exact before → after ("রংধনু ০ → ১", "ঝিকমিক ০ → ২", "চাল ২৫ → ২৮") and one marked **এটা ভালো হবে** (more moves when the bot needs more than 62% of them, else a রংধনু). A fourth line starts without. A gift is help only: the extra moves add no bonus points. Links carry it (`p=`).
  - **সব লেভেল:** four মেলা of ten: name, new thing, stars, finales marked শেষ ধাপ, locks, **এটা খেলো** on the recommended level; keys 1–9. Also in the player's menu.
  - **End screen:** ১ পরের লেভেল (এটাই ভালো) · ২ আবার খেলো · ৩ সব লেভেল; keys 1–3 and Enter, fresh presses only.
- **HUD (corners only, the tray stays clear):** top-left the level badge (opens সব লেভেল); top-centre the goals as bangle pictures with how many are left (a ✓ and a green border when done); top-right the moves left, with the points under it. A finale adds a boss row under the HUD (its name, a red bar that drains as the goals fill). One status line sits under the tray (the lesson, "পরপর ×৩", the shake, the red-ring warning, the last three moves). The streak never covers the tray.
- **Danger at the edges:** when the moves left are ≤ max(4, 20%) the tray's edge glows amber, and ≤ 2 red and faster; a still border in ধীরে খেলো. A moves-left tick sounds at 3, 2 and 1. A red ring with an arrow marks the cell the next তালা will fall on, a move ahead.
- **Finales:** the boss row, the মেলা's own difficulty at its strongest, a gift choice before it, the মেলা's three-note call on the clear, confetti on the first clear, a red wax stamp on the card.
- **End screen:** the kicker ("লেভেল ৭ পার", "বড় বায়না: পার", "চাল শেষ"), the stars popping in, stats counting up (the score longest): চাল, পয়েন্ট, চুড়ি তুলেছ, সেরা পরপর, ঝিকমিক + রংধনু, তালা খুলেছ, ধুলো মুছেছ, সময়; the run's biggest single move ("৫ নম্বর চালে এক চালেই ৪৮টা চুড়ি উঠেছে"), the line, the exact near-miss ("আর ২০ পয়েন্ট হলে ৩ তারা", or on a loss "আর ৩টা নীল চুড়ি হলেই হতো"), the best on this phone, the numbered choices. **কার্ড দেখো ↓** scrolls to the card.
- **Card:** the velvet stall with a rod of bangles per colour (the goal colours stacked tallest), the stars, the name and points, and on a finale a red stamp with its name. Links: `#s=<seed>&t=<points>&v=2&l=<level>&m=<moves>&d=<mode>&p=<gift>&n=<name>`.

## Sound (`components/sound.ts`, only `shared/game-sound.ts`; every note of a move is laid on the audio clock with `tone(…, delay)` so a pause or the next level cuts it)
1. **Pick and swap:** a small glass tick on the press, a soft tick on the swap. You see the highlight and the slide.
2. **Clink:** a glass chime with a low thock under it so a lift has weight, one pentatonic step higher for each step of a cascade (you see the pop and the floating points).
3. **Shatter:** a bright rush and two falling bits when four or more lift (you see the shards).
4. **Specials:** a four-note shimmer for ঝিকমিক, a seven-note sweep for রংধনু, a whoosh for a blast (you see the ring and the gold lanes).
5. **Locks and dust:** a clack and a chain when a তালা falls, a lock opening when one is freed, a soft brush for dust (you see the padlock and the wipe).
6. **Moves low:** one thin tick at 3, 2, 1 (you see the red edge).
- **Signature:** রিনিঝিনি, a wrist of glass bangles, on a clear and when the tray shakes.
- **Loop:** the rattle, only while the tray shakes with sound on. It stops on pause, blur, hide, the end, unmount and when the shake ends; it comes back when sound turns on or play resumes mid-shake.

## Comfort and controls
- **ধীরে খেলো:** swaps take 70 ms and pops 70 ms; no shards, rings, board nudge, glints, pulse, bump, count-up, confetti or entrance animation; the danger edge and the hint are still borders. The star scores and limits are unchanged. `prefers-reduced-motion` turns it on by default.
- **Touch and mouse:** press a bangle, drag it 30% of a cell towards a neighbour and the swap happens at that moment (not on lift); or tap one, then the pair. A press during an animation skips it and counts. A mouse that moves with no button held does nothing.
- **Keys:** arrows move a cursor; Enter or Space picks up the bangle under it; an arrow then swaps (no auto-repeat); P opens the player's menu; 1–3 in the gift and on the end screen; Enter or Space starts and picks the first choice (fresh presses only). Escape belongs to the player.
- **Away:** blur, a hidden tab and every choice screen stop input, the hint timer and the rattle; a press in the first 250 ms after চালিয়ে যাও or after the window comes back is ignored, so the click that brings focus back is never a move.
- **Kept for compatibility:**
  - old links `#s=&t=&l=&m=&n=` (no `v`) still parse and replay the old level table exactly (`lib/legacy.ts`, pinned by `tests/legacy-fixture.json` recorded from the old code); they open as "বন্ধুর পুরোনো লেভেল" and record nothing. New links add `v=2`, `d=` and `p=`;
  - stars in `ektukhani-levels-churir-bazar` keep their level numbers; the best per level moves to `ektukhani-best-churir-bazar-v2-l<N>` (the old bests stay where they are);
  - no tally; `/churir-bazar`, its title, H1 and slug stay, and the description is the same sentence with the new things named.
